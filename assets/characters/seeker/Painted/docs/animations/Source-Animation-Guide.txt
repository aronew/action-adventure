HOODED SEEKER - ANIMATION GUIDE  (PRO)
Primitive Pixels

This is a quick doc rundown that you can reference if you ever get stuck
trying to figure out how to rig up the animations or lining up VFX to frames.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TWO THINGS TO KNOW FIRST
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE FEET POINT (PIVOT)
   The frames are different sizes, because a sword swing needs more
   room than standing still. Every animation has a "feet" point: the
   spot on the ground between his feet, counted in pixels from the
   top-left corner of a frame. Set that as the sprite's origin and
   every animation lines up, whatever its size.

2. READING A SHEET
   Every sheet is one row of frames, left to right. The frame size and
   the number of frames are in the file name: Seeker_Void_Run_30x30_10f.png
   means each frame is 30 x 30 and there are 10 frames.
   <Palette> in a file name below means Void, Ember, Frost, Bone
   or Dream.

THE SPELL CASTS (PRO)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Each cast comes two ways on the same 64 x 48 canvas:
  - the full sheet, with the magic already drawn in, or
  - a Body sheet plus an FX sheet (in FX Split/), drawn on top of
    each other, same frame number. Use these to recolour, glow or
    hide the magic.
The flying projectile is never drawn into the cast, because it has
to move in your game. Spawn it on the frame listed below.

----------------------------------------------------------------
THE VFX
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every effect is one row of frames and plays at 20 fps (50 ms a frame).

To use one:
1. Pick the point in your world where it happens (his hand, the
   impact, his feet).
2. Put the effect's anchor pixel on that point. The anchor is listed
   below, counted from the top-left of a frame, and drawn in pink in
   VFX/Seeker_VFX_Guide.png.
3. Play it once, or keep looping it if it says loops, then remove it.

Shots, muzzle flashes and Dust_Dash are drawn facing right. Flip them
to face left, around the anchor.

Tip: they look great as they are. For extra glow, draw a second copy
with additive blending on top at about half opacity.
