Canon levels (per `.claude/rules/narrative.md`): **Established** = stated in the source games, per the sources. **Provisional** = our best answer, the owner may change it. **Under Review** = sources disagree or the point was not verified. All names below are **placeholders**.

## Direction (owner, 2026-11-22)

- Tone and setting: a fallen kingdom, hushed and haunted (Hollow Knight tone).
- Structure: three acts with non-linear regions inside each act, as in Silksong, not one region after another.
- Kept from the current design: the Seeker (Bone palette) and the guard-break combat. Kiln City's cast, names, lore and story are dropped in this version.

## Premise

Vesperhold, a mountain kingdom far beyond Hallownest and Pharloom, fell in a single night. Its dead and living still walk the halls asleep, fixed in their last duties. The Seeker, a mind that cannot be put to sleep, is sent to wake what dreams under the mountain.

## World rules

| Rule | Entry | Level |
|---|---|---|
| The fall | Vesperhold fell in one night when its three Wardens of Sleep, living seals around something that dreams under the mountain, failed. Modelled on the Dreamers as seals | Provisional |
| The held dream | Since the fall, the sleeper's dream walks the kingdom. Wardens, courtiers and guards are fixed in their last duties and fight on in their sleep. This held dream is the **guard** of bosses and mini-bosses | Provisional |
| Who has a guard | Only the dream-held: bosses, mini-bosses and the one tutorial foe. Ordinary creatures are frightened and hurt, with no guard | Provisional (matches the brief's guard rule) |
| Breaking a guard | A sudden, unfiltered flash wakes the sleeper for a moment and cracks the hold. The charged heavy is that flash | Provisional |
| The Seeker | An empty, Vessel-like being the last queen hid in the vault: a mind that cannot be put to sleep. It cannot dream, so the held dream has no grip on it, which is why it can break a guard. Depends on the Vessel row above | Provisional, Under Review |
| Palettes | Bone = the Seeker as found, unfinished and unwoken. Void, Ember and Frost are later empowered states. Dream Seeker = a late state in which the Seeker dreams for the first time | Provisional |
| What the Seeker takes from a Keeper | Not decided here. Kiln City's rule ("it soaks in the craft a guard was carrying") may be kept, changed or dropped | Open |

## Structure: three acts, non-linear inside each

The owner asked for Silksong's three-act structure. The ability breakdown the owner pasted for Silksong (Act 1: Swift Step, Cling Grip and others; Act 2: Clawline, Faydown Cloak and others; Act 3: Silk Soar and others) was **not verified by us** and is recorded only as the model for the shape.

| Act | Regions | Abilities | How it opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1: The Outer Halls** | 3 regions, any order | Dash (from a mini-boss), wall cling (from a boss) | Each region needs one key or none, so the player picks a route |
| **2: The Deep Court** | 3 regions, any order | Grapple, double jump | Same shape; the Act 1 keys are assumed |
| **3: The Dreaming Mountain** | 2 regions | The Dream Seeker state; bow and spells as optional late tools | The Seeker dreams for the first time, which is how it can reach the sleeper |

The ability order here differs from the current ability-gating GDD (dash, double jump, wall cling, grapple, set 2026-11-20 in the repo and tied to a linear region order). The brief's ability rules and the grid map assume that order.
