# Region 1: The Cooling Yards

Status: **Revision awaiting owner approval** (2026-11-18). The approved version had region 1 earn only the dash; this revision moves Tavik, Brun and Dorrit's jar to match the level design GDD's key mapping (owner decision 2026-11-18). Lore text is unchanged except the provisional tutorial foe. Drafts and history in `production/narrative/`. Identity: the long yards behind the kilns where fired work was carried to cool, now a street of glazed couriers still running.

**Owner decisions in force:** region 1 earns the dash, from Keeper Tavik, its mid-region mini-boss, and the wall cling, from Dorrit's jar, behind a dash gate. Its boss is Brun, the Stacker, beyond a dash gate and a wall-cling gate. Double jump and grapple are SEEN here (each gate at least twice), lead to sealed alcoves in the core build, and are earned in later regions. One tutorial foe in the first minute is the only other guarded creature (level design rule 20).

## Places (what the player sees, not reads)

1. **Waking Niche** (start, save). A cooling niche in a kiln wall, brick warm on the back. The Seeker's own shape pressed in the ash of the floor.
2. **Yard Steps.** Glazed figures stand still along the wall, one mid-sneeze. High on a far roofline, one small steady window-light no kiln would make (a lure toward region 2).
3. **Saggar Rows.** Columns of stacked clay boxes to the ceiling, a ladder lashed to each, a chalk "B" on every tally. A single flat kiln shelf hangs high on three chalk stilts.
4. **Yard Gate** (first dash gap). A broken road. Orange soot streaks run to each lip of the gap, as if something has crossed it a thousand times.
5. **Carrier's Road.** A long street of glazed couriers leaning one way, trays held out, a pot still glowing on each. An iron ring hangs far above, out of reach.
6. **Carrier's Kiln** (mini-boss Keeper). A cold hearth room, a table of empty trays, one cooling niche with a pot waiting on the sill.
7. **Flue Run** (shortcut). The Carrier's Kiln's back flue, sooted clean, folding back to the Yard Gate niche.
8. **Dorrit's Bench** (shrine, behind the Yard Gate's dash gap). Dorrit Kell's corner of the flue-sweeps' yard: stool, apron on a peg, a tall jar with the last coil unlaid.
9. **Saggar Hall** (boss). Brun's quarter at the top of the rows: a hall of saggar columns to the roof, one of them still being built.
10. **Loft Way** (edge toward region 2, past the Saggar Hall). A passage to a quarter of stacked halls. The steady lamp is nearer here.
11. **Ropewalk Stair** (edge toward later regions). A stair into a wide drop. An iron ring across the gap, a violet cord trailing from it, and below, rope-wound doors shut.

## The mini-boss Keeper: Tavik, the Hot-Carrier

- **Who:** head of the carriers, who ran fresh-fired work from kiln to cooling niche before it cracked. Glazed mid-run on the last evening, a pot on his tray he never set down.
- **Craft of passage:** the hot carry, the last few steps when the tray cannot be set down. It becomes the dash.
- **Fight:** short hot bursts tray-first, then stops dead at the end of each. That stop is the opening.
- **When he falls:** the pots on the Road's trays go dark one by one, the soot streaks go cold, and the back flue opens. The couriers keep standing. No banner.

## The boss: Brun, the Stacker

- **Who:** glazed loader in oxblood, a saggar column on his back, still stacking. He taught Pell.
- **Fight:** swings and drops the column in sweeps. Has a glaze guard.
- **When he falls:** the column topples into a ramp (the arena's shortcut back down). No banner.

## The shrine: Dorrit's jar

Dorrit Kell swept the tall flues and made jars on the side. She never glazed and died unglazed mid-coil. Stool, apron on a peg, a tall jar with the last coil unlaid, a left palm-print in its wall. The Seeker lays its palm into her palm-print and steadies the wall of the jar. The clay takes the hand: wall cling.

## Save point: the cooling niche

The Seeker sits, back to warm brick. Ash settles. Its pale shade warms a half-step and the nearest glazed figure's highlight slowly dims. It stands up the same as it sat down.

## Ordinary creatures (no guard, health only)

- **Wasters:** lopsided failed pieces, hopping jugs and slumped bowls. Never fired through or glazed, so they chip and die like clay.
- **Cinder moths:** ash-grey moths drawn to the flue-light. Living things; no skin to hold.

- **Half-glazed Waster** (the tutorial foe, first minute; canon: *Provisional*, owner to confirm): the one exception to the Wasters above, a failed piece that was carried into a firing by mistake and came out with a thin, crazed skin of glaze. Its glaze is weak, and the first charged heavy shatters it. It appears once and never returns.

Glazed figures that are not Keepers or mini-bosses are inert scenery. In region 1 only Tavik, Brun and the half-glazed Waster wear glaze and move.

## In region 2

- **Pell, the mentor** (double jump, the pact): the Stacking Loft.

- **Pell:** stooped loader, leather apron, cloth-wrapped hands, bad hip, a small lamp of real flame, a cup with a thumb-dent. Three facts: he stacked under Brun; he never glazed and is wearing down (hairline cracks under the wraps); he has seen the Seeker's shape before in Teodra's workshop sketches. Promise hidden (see `lore-premise.md`): he will one day ask for something and the Seeker is to refuse. Double jump is the loader's second step.
