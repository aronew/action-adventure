Status: **Draft, Provisional.** Written 2026-11-22 at the owner's request, as an alternative to `premise.md` (Kiln City), which is untouched and remains the backup. This file layers on Kiln City: its names, regions, art direction and level design stay valid; only the *explanation* of glaze, the Seeker and the Void changes.
Canon levels (per `.claude/rules/narrative.md`): **Established** = stated in the source games, per the sources listed. **Provisional** = our best answer, owner may change. **Under Review** = sources disagree or the point was not verified.

## Premise

Far beyond Hallownest and Pharloom, a city of potters glazed themselves against the end. The Seeker is a Void-bodied Vessel, cast out of the Abyss, sent down the long road to let the kilns go cold.

## What changes from Kiln City

| Kiln City entry | In the spin-off | Level |
|---|---|---|
| Glaze: a fired skin that seals the body | Glaze is fired with **lightfire**: a trace of the Radiance's forgotten light, carried to the potters in a trade-lamp. It seals the body in an endless dream, which is why the glazed never age | Provisional |
| Guard of bosses, mini-bosses and the tutorial foe | A **held dream**: the glaze fixes them to their last task. Ordinary enemies are not glazed, so they have no guard | Provisional |
| Crazing: sudden heat or cold cracks glaze | A sudden, unfiltered flash wakes the dreamer and cracks the dream-glaze. The charged heavy is that flash | Provisional |
| The Seeker: bisque, never glazed, porous | A **Void-bodied Vessel**. It cannot dream, so the dream has no grip on it, which is why it can break a guard. Its body soaks in the craft a Keeper was carrying, as the porous bisque did | Provisional |
| Palettes as firings (Bone, Ember, Frost, Void) | Bone = the unwoken Seeker. Void = the Void in the body. Ember and Frost stay firings. Dream Seeker = a later state in which the Seeker dreams for the first time | Provisional |
| The end: the Dry | Unchanged | Established (Kiln City) |
| Banned-trope guard: no pantheon, no prophecy | **Conflict.** A Radiance trace and the Void make this a pantheon-adjacent story. Either drop the guard or keep both as rumour only | Under Review |

## Cast (kept from Kiln City)

- **Teodra:** last unglazed master; found the Seeker in the cold and sent it on the errand. Now dust. Level: Provisional.
- **Pell:** journeyman, never glazed because he was afraid. End scene open (the core build never reaches it). Level: Established (Kiln City).
- **Hesk:** fired the first glaze over her dying daughter Ilo to hold her until morning. It held. She stands glazed in a later region; not a final boss. Level: Established (Kiln City).
- **Keepers (Tavik, Brun, others):** moving on because their last task is still running. The Seeker soaks in the craft each was carrying, which gives it the four abilities. Level: Established (Kiln City).

## Arc, 8 regions

1. Region 1 (Cinderheart Caldera): dash, from Keeper Tavik; the region ends with Brun.
2. Region 2: double jump. Region 3: wall cling. Region 4: grapple. Each comes from a Keeper's last craft.
3. Regions 5-8: the long descent toward the kilns. The lightfire grows stronger, and by the end the Seeker is dreaming.
4. The hint that this city's lightfire came down the same road as Hallownest's forgotten light appears only in later regions.
