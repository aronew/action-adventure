## HUD data contract

The HUD (R9) has no GDD. Until it has one, Healing publishes this and nothing else; the HUD reads, never writes.

| Item | Type | Owner | Updated | Notes |
|------|------|-------|---------|-------|
| `heal_meter`, `meter_capacity` | float | the meter component | on the tick a fill, a spend, a reset or a capacity change happens | the HUD redraws on change, never on a timer |
| `heal_progress` | float 0 to 1 | the channel | every tick of a channel; frozen while paused | 0 outside a channel |
| `usable` | bool | the meter component | whenever the meter, health or max health changes | true when the meter is full and missing health is at least `start_deficit`; it does not depend on the Seeker's state, so a banked bar in a state that blocks the heal still reads usable (the press is the only refusal) |
| `meter_full(cause)` | signal | the meter component | the tick the bar reaches capacity | cause `hit` plays the ready tone and pulse; any other cause (a capacity change) does not |
| `heal_started`, `heal_completed(effective)`, `heal_cancelled(reason)`, `heal_refused(reason)` | signals | the channel | the tick of the event | reasons are listed in AC-39 of the GDD |
| Meter change cause | enum on each change | the meter component | with each change | `hit`, `rescale`, `reset`, `spend`; the fill pulse plays for `hit` only |

## Visual and audio requirements

The `art-director` lens was applied in-session against `design/art/art-bible.md` sections 1-4; the `audio-director` reviewed this section in the fifth review, and audio is direction only (Audio is index #25, Not Started).

**Art bible principles that apply.** *Principle 3, Combat owns the light:* no heal effect may out-shine the sword arc or the guard-break spark. *Principle 2, greyscale order:* the Seeker is the lightest thing in the room; the heal glow raises the Seeker, never what is around it. *Principle 1:* effects on the Seeker are pixel art at 4x with nearest filtering. *Section 4:* warm and pale is "you" (Bone Pale), steady Kiln Amber is "safe", cool ice-white is "an enemy attack to dodge"; the four key colours belong to abilities. The heal uses none of the last three.

| Event | Visual | Audio (direction only) | Limits |
|-------|--------|------------------------|--------|
| A hit fills the meter | On the meter only: it brightens at the new fill edge and settles in about 0.15 s. Nothing in the world | None; the hit's own sound carries the moment | Not rate-limited today; **counts toward `flash_limit`** and is part of AC-27's scenarios (a combo can fire it more than 3 times a second, so it is a brightness-neutral edge move, not a luminance event) |
| The meter becomes full (**banked**) | One soft pulse on the Seeker (its pale shade warms a half-step and eases back) and the meter's banked state on the HUD. **While a heal is usable** (meter full and missing health at least `start_deficit`) the Seeker also carries one small, steady, non-colour marker: a silhouette notch or rim in Bone Pale **with a Void Black outline or halo**, at least 2 source pixels thick, no animation. Contrast is judged against its own dark outline, because Bone Pale (luminance about 0.36) cannot reach 3:1 against the lighter floor lips. It goes out when the meter is spent or health rises above the threshold. A banked bar with health too high shows a distinct **banked, not usable** HUD state only | A short **fixed-pitch chime**, not a rising sweep (a sweep would read as a boss charge-up) and not a variant of the refusal tick; audible over boss music; the hit's own sound wins when they coincide | Pulse eased over at least 0.5 s, once per 0-to-full crossing caused by a landed hit (never on overflow, revive, load, room entry or rest); never a flicker. Peak no brighter than Bone Pale (luma 0.64); the marker is steady and never counts as a flash |
| `heal` refused, by reason | **(a) Meter not full:** the meter dims and shudders about 0.2 s. **(b) Missing health below `start_deficit`:** the end of the health display nudges once over about 0.3 s (a positional shift, no brightness change). **(c) A movement or combat state:** no cue (only when meter and health would allow the heal). Plus a **brief shape echo on the Seeker** on every refused (a) or (b) press so a press that did nothing is seen to do nothing | (a) a dull, short tick; (b) a soft, short tick, higher than (a) | Never red. **Every refused press emits `heal_refused` and gets the non-luminance echo within 1 tick.** The limiters live in presentation: **at most one luminance cue per 30 ticks (0.5 s)** and **at most one audio tick per 9 ticks (0.15 s)**, counted in integer ticks, shared across reasons. With Flash reduction On the refusal cue is never a luminance change. Never played during a door transition, a rest or death |
| Channel (1.0 s) | A planted heal pose; its own warm light grows steadily with `heal_progress`; a few crisp pixel motes rise into it, one at each quarter (0.25, 0.5, 0.75), **the 0.5 mark carrying a visible tick that is the twin of the audio pip**, so progress reads without the glow. The glow must change the Seeker's brightness by a visible step (a minimum luma delta, set in the first story and checked in AC-31), because the body is already 0.53 to 0.61 | A soft swell whose loudness rises with `heal_progress` (not a pitch sweep; low, narrow band), with a discrete pip at 0.5 s | **Bone Pale, never Kiln Amber, never ice-white, never a key colour.** Steady, nothing flickers. **Requirement, not a recipe:** the glow is capped at Bone Pale (luma 0.64) whatever the sprite shows under it; it is not multiplied by the heavy-ready flash and does not follow the invulnerability blink; and it cannot add above the cap. How it is drawn is the implementation notes' |
| Completion | One soft bloom on the Seeker as health refills, easing out over about 0.4 s; the HUD health fills | A warm, low swell that settles | Peak no brighter than Bone Pale, never Flash White (0.93). **A completion at max health is silent** (it is a `full_health` cancel) |
| Cancelled | The glow fades over at least 0.3 s with no flash while control returns at once; a hit's own hurt effect plays over it | A **30 to 80 ms fade** of the swell (a one-tick cut clicks), one voice for the swell so rapid start and cancel never overlap tails; **no cancel sound on a hit** (it would mask the hurt sound); a minimal one on a release (a slip is free) | A fall of 10% luminance or more counts toward the flash limit, so the fade is never faster than the limit allows |

**Flash limit.** The pulse, the bloom, the refusal luminance cue, the cancel fade, the glow and the meter's fill pulse count toward registry `flash_limit` (3 per second, WCAG 2.3.1) **together**, with the invulnerability blink and the hurt effect on the same screen area measured in the same capture. A luminance event is a pair of opposing changes of at least 0.10 relative luminance (absolute, WCAG's reading); the registry states the limit as "10% relative luminance" and the unit is to be confirmed against `pause-and-menus.md` F4 (listed under Open items below). With Flash reduction On the heal's lights read as steady glows and the refusal cue is a positional nudge. HDR output must be off or capped, and the renderer setting is recorded with the capture, because with HDR on a value above 1.0 can display brighter than SDR white. With the atmosphere set to Reduced (parallax rule 14) the heal still reads through the pose, the marker and the meter, not through bloom.

**Audio event contract and owner.** Gameplay behaviour, fixed here: the ready tone fires only on a 0-to-full crossing caused by a landed hit; the swell starts on `heal_started`, follows `heal_progress` (so a pause freezes it, and it never follows `time_scale` or pitch) and stops within 80 ms of every exit except completion, where it hands over to the completion swell (one voice, so the tails never overlap); every heal audio cue has a visual twin and none is the only carrier of information. The channel swell must not share the shape of a boss attack charge-up, so Bosses owes a transient-onset telegraph sound outside the swell's band (art bible l.120 already requires enemy telegraphs to carry a sound; `bosses.md` has none: owed by Bosses, listed under Open items below). Owed to the Audio GDD (index #25, Open Question 19): mix priority and ducking, ready-tone anti-fatigue (it can fire every 10 s in a boss fight), a sound per cancel exit, loudness targets, bus and naming, the audio acceptance criteria.

**Animation.** `seeker_animations.json` has no heal clip. New clips `heal_loop` (planted pose, loops) and `heal_end` (cosmetic: control returns on the completion tick and any input interrupts it). Frame counts are the animator's, never tied to `channel_time`. Build details: the implementation notes.

**HUD colours** (for the HUD GDD): the meter and its full state use no key colour and no red against green; the full state is a shape or brightness cue, not a hue change alone.

## UI requirements

- **A heal meter in the HUD** near the health display: a bar (no number by default **[Rec]**; a tick or percentage option for low-vision players is the HUD GDD's, listed under Open items below) with high-contrast marks at empty, half and full, a clear **banked** state, a distinct **banked, not usable** state (full bar, health too high), and the refusal cues by reason. The full state is a brightness and shape change, not a hue alone. The usable state is the marker on the Seeker (Visual/Audio); in a stagger that marker is the signal that a heal is available, and no extra readout is added **[Rec]**.
- **Hold or toggle (accessibility).** `heal_mode` (hold, default, or toggle) on an options page (Open Question 14), recorded in `design/accessibility-requirements.md`. Toggle is the accommodation for players who cannot hold an input for a full second; in toggle mode a stick push also cancels, so those players are never trapped planted.
- **Channel progress** shown on the Seeker (the growing glow, the pose, the motes), not as a second bar.
- **The controls page** (Pause and menus, read-only) lists `heal`: **keyboard A and gamepad B** (Open Question 1; B replaced Y, owner 2026-11-23).
- The HUD's look waits for art bible section 7 and the HUD GDD (R9).

## Presentation acceptance criteria (moved from the GDD, numbers kept)

- **AC-25 Meter display [UI, needs the HUD GDD].** A retained screenshot shows the meter at 0, 60 of 120, banked-and-usable, banked-not-usable and each refusal family. A still cannot show a 0.2 s shudder, so AC-27 covers its motion.
- **AC-27 Flash limit and cue limiters [27a Logic; 27b to 27e UI, retained capture].** **27a** ten refused presses at ticks 0, 6, 12, ..., 54: ten `heal_refused` signals, **exactly 2 luminance cues (ticks 0 and 30)** and **exactly 5 audio ticks (0, 12, 24, 36, 48)**, counted in integer ticks. **27b** a scripted second with 10 refused presses, and a second with 5 start-and-cancel cycles (presses at ticks 0, 12, 24, 36, 48, each released 6 ticks later), plus a combo of 6 hits filling the meter, captured at 60 fps in a rendering run (not headless): in any 1 s window at most 3 luminance events occur counting the refusal cue, glow, pulse, bloom, fade, the meter's fill pulse, the invulnerability blink and the hurt effect together. **27c** with Flash reduction On the same captures show 0 luminance events. **27d** the pulse and bloom peak at luma 0.64 or less and never reach 0.93. **27e** the glow is never Kiln Amber, ice-white or a key colour. *Measurement:* the region is the Seeker's sprite rectangle plus the meter; the 27d caps use the art bible's luma; the event count uses WCAG relative luminance (an event is a pair of opposing changes of at least 0.10); HDR output is off; the renderer setting is recorded. Sign-off by the lead.
- **AC-31 Heal visuals [UI, needs the heal clip].** Retained screenshots show the ready pulse and the persistent marker (with its Void Black outline at the measured contrast), the channel glow at ticks 0, 30 and 59 with a visible luma delta on the body, the completion bloom and the `heal_end` settle, each within the art bible's colour limits, lead sign-off.
- **AC-40 Refusal and echo [Logic].** Every refused (a) or (b) press gets the non-luminance echo event within 1 tick; the (c) family gets none.
