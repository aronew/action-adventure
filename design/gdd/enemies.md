## Overview

A normal enemy is an `EnemyBase` (a `CharacterBody2D`) configured by an `EnemyConfig` resource: the three types (Grunt, Skitter, Brute) are one script with three `.tres` files. An enemy patrols, notices the Seeker, chases it, winds up a telegraphed melee attack, strikes, recovers, and repeats. It has health only (no guard: concept anti-pillar 1). Every attack gives the player a wind-up to read and dodge, and touching an enemy never hurts. The boss builds on the same base.

**Design intent**
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** **no contact damage.** Enemies and the Seeker pass through each other; every damaging attack is telegraphed. This is the rule for all enemies (concept: readable, telegraphed attacks; pillar 1).
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** Grunt, Skitter and Brute are **behaviour archetypes** (plain, fast, heavy with no flinch). The lore enemies (the half-glazed Wasters and others) reuse them as reskins or variants of the same base and config. The art is placeholder boxes today.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the **leash and the spawn-point return are requirements**: the level-design GDD (rule 24, F7) and the registry (`chase_leash` 48 px) stand, and the code is behind. See Edge Cases.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** **only named foes stay dead**: the tutorial Waster, mini-bosses and bosses, recorded in world state. Ordinary enemies return when their room is re-entered. Neither is built.
- `INTENT UNKNOWN — inferred from implementation, not confirmed`: the per-type numbers (they are tuning, set in the `.tres` files), the 5% game slowdown on a killing blow, and sight with no line-of-sight check.

**Scope**: in: the state machine, patrol, sight, chase, the attack cycle, being hit, death, the three types, the contract with the Seeker and combat. Out: the guard, the boss's patterns, art and audio, room placement and persistence (Level design, Rooms, World state).

## Detailed Design

### States and transitions

`TRANSITIONS` is the whole table (state → event → next state); an event not listed changes nothing and `DEAD` has no way out.

| State | Behaviour | Events out |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| `PATROL` | Walks between two points around a home x, pausing at each end; stands still when `patrol_speed` is 0 | `PLAYER_SEEN` → CHASE |
| `CHASE` | Runs at the Seeker; stops at ledges; stands when in attack range | `PLAYER_LOST` → PATROL; `IN_RANGE` → TELEGRAPH |
| `TELEGRAPH` | The wind-up: stands still; the attack cannot hurt yet | `TELEGRAPH_ENDED` → ATTACK |
| `ATTACK` | Hitbox live for a short window with a lunge, then recovery | `ATTACK_ENDED` → CHASE |
| `HURT` | Stagger: no moving, no attacking; a hit restarts it | `HURT_ENDED` → CHASE |
| `STAGGER` | Guard broken (guarded foes only); lasts the guard's stagger time | `STAGGER_ENDED` → CHASE |
| `DEAD` | Falls over, fades, is freed | none |

`HIT` sends PATROL, CHASE, TELEGRAPH, ATTACK and HURT to HURT, except that during TELEGRAPH and ATTACK a config with `flinch_during_attack = false` takes the damage but ignores the stagger. `GUARD_BROKEN` and `KILLED` reach every state except DEAD (STAGGER takes only `KILLED`).

### Rules

1. **Patrol.** The home x is the spawn x at first. The enemy walks at `patrol_speed` until it is `patrol_distance` from home, a wall, or a ledge (a 4 px floor probe ahead), then waits `patrol_pause_time` and turns. With `patrol_speed` 0 it stands guard.
2. **Sight.** From PATROL, the enemy notices the Seeker when `|dx| ≤ sight_range` and `|dy| ≤ sight_height`. There is no line-of-sight or facing check. A dead Seeker is never noticed.
3. **Chase.** It faces the Seeker and runs at `chase_speed` until `|dx| > lose_range` (horizontal only) or the Seeker is dead, then returns to PATROL. It does not run off a ledge, does not jump, and does not pursue vertically.
4. **Attack.** In CHASE, when `|dx| ≤ attack_range`, the vertical gap is within `sight_height` and the cooldown is clear, it starts a wind-up. The body heats to orange and leans back, the attack's reach fills in, and a "!" shows (the telegraph). After `telegraph_time` the hitbox is live for `attack_active_time`, the enemy lunging at `attack_lunge_speed` (no lunge off a ledge), then recovers for `attack_recovery_time` and waits `attack_cooldown` before the next wind-up. The hitbox is mirrored with facing, which is fixed during the attack.
5. **Damage to the Seeker.** One hit per attack: `Player.take_hit(attack_damage, position)`; the Seeker's invulnerability window applies.
6. **Being hit.** Each Seeker hit on the enemy's hurtbox reduces health by the damage (no armour). The enemy is staggered for `hurt_time`, pushed away from the Seeker at `hurt_knockback_speed` (plus a hop of `hurt_knockback_lift` on the ground), and the push bleeds off at `hurt_deceleration`. Its body flashes and recoils.
7. **Death.** At zero health: it is launched away (`death_knockback_speed`, `death_lift_speed`), `died` is emitted once, the hurtbox leaves the `hittable` group, the whole game slows to `death_hitstop_scale` (0.05) for `death_hitstop_time` real seconds, the camera shakes, fragments burst, the body topples (0.25 s) and fades over `death_time`, then it is freed.
8. **Guard.** Only an enemy given a `guard_config` has a guard (bosses and mini-bosses); normal enemies have none.

### The three types (`.tres`)

| Value | Grunt | Skitter | Brute |
|-------|------:|--------:|------:|
| Role | plain | fast | heavy hitter |
| Body (w x h) | 14 x 24 | 12 x 14 | 22 x 34 |
| `max_health` | 85 | 50 | 220 |
| `patrol_speed` / `patrol_distance` / pause | 30 / 48 / 0.8 s | 60 / 64 / 0.4 s | 0 (stands guard) |
| `chase_speed` | 70 | 150 | 45 |
| `sight_range` / `sight_height` / `lose_range` | 140 / 40 / 240 | 180 / 40 / 280 | 150 / 50 / 260 |
| `attack_range` | 26 | 70 | 34 |
| `telegraph_time` / `attack_active_time` | 0.5 / 0.12 s | 0.35 / 0.25 s | 0.9 / 0.15 s |
| `attack_recovery_time` / `attack_cooldown` | 0.4 / 0.8 s | 0.5 / 1.0 s | 0.8 / 1.2 s |
| `attack_damage` | 15 | 10 | 30 |
| `attack_lunge_speed` | 60 | 280 | 40 |
| `hurt_time` | 0.35 s | 0.3 s | 0.2 s |
| `hurt_knockback_speed` / lift / deceleration | 150 / 70 / 700 | 190 / 90 / 700 | 65 / 0 / 500 |
| `flinch_during_attack` | true | true | **false** |
| Death: hit-stop, shake, fragments | 0.08 s, 3 px, 14 | 0.07 s, 2 px, 10 | 0.14 s, 5 px, 24 |

All three use gravity 900 and a fall cap of 480.

### Contract with other systems

- **Layers.** The enemy body is on layer 16 and collides with the world only; its hurtbox is on layer 8 in group `hittable` (so every enemy is also a pogo target); its attack hitbox is on no layer and looks for the Seeker on layer 4.
- **Setup.** `config` and `target` are set before entering the tree (both asserted). The enemy listens to the target's `PlayerCombat.hit_landed`.
- **Signals.** `damaged(amount, health_left)`, `state_changed(from, to)`, `died(enemy)`.

## Formulas

**F1. Hit on an enemy.** `health = max(health - damage, 0)`; with a guard, `damage` is the guard's returned amount (see `guard-break.md`).

**F2. Knockback travel.** `knockback_travel = hurt_knockback_speed² / (2 x hurt_deceleration)`: Grunt 16 px, Skitter 26 px, Brute 4 px. (`level-design.md` quotes 4-26 px for the current types.)

**F3. Lunge travel.** `lunge_travel = lunge_speed x attack_active_time + lunge_speed² / (2 x hurt_deceleration)`: Grunt 7.2 + 2.6 = 10 px, Skitter 70 + 56 = 126 px, Brute 6 + 1.6 = 8 px. (`level-design.md` quotes 8-126 px.)

**F4. Attack cycle.** `cycle = telegraph_time + attack_active_time + attack_recovery_time`, then `attack_cooldown` before the next wind-up: Grunt 1.02 s + 0.8 s, Skitter 1.10 s + 1.0 s, Brute 1.85 s + 1.2 s. The player's reaction window is `telegraph_time`: 0.5 / 0.35 / 0.9 s.

**F5. Observed context, not a rule.** Against the Seeker's 100 health, one hit costs Grunt 15, Skitter 10, Brute 30 (7, 10 and 4 landed hits). A full melee combo (42) does not kill a Skitter (50).

## Edge Cases

**Handled in the code**
- Events not in the table are ignored; a hit during HURT restarts the stagger and the knockback.
- A hit on a dead enemy is ignored; a dead enemy leaves the `hittable` group at once.
- An enemy never walks or lunges off a ledge; in the air the floor probe reports "floor ahead", so a falling enemy is not blocked.
- A dead or freed Seeker ends the chase (`PLAYER_LOST`) and blocks noticing.
- The attack lands at most once per wind-up even if the hitbox overlaps the Seeker for the whole window.
- A killing blow on any state, including mid-attack, goes to DEAD and cancels the hitbox.

**Required by other GDDs, not built**
- **Leash and return** (level design rule 24 and F7, registry `chase_leash` 48 px; **confirmed required by the owner**): a chasing ground enemy is clamped to `spawn_x ± (patrol_distance + chase_leash)` (grown by half its width; a vertical strip at every height), including against hit knockback and attack lunge, and walks back to its **spawn point** when it loses the Seeker. **The code does the opposite**: on entering PATROL it re-homes (`_home_x = global_position.x`), so a chase moves the patrol area. The leash bounds are also what the gate-safety rules in `ability-gating.md` and `level-design.md` assume.
- **Return delay** (rooms-and-transitions, registry `enemy_return_delay` 2 s): an enemy knocked out of its room through an open bottom edge or a pit is removed at the room bounds and reappears at its spawn point after 2 s. Nothing removes or respawns an enemy in the code.
- **Persistence** (world-state; **confirmed by the owner**): the tutorial Waster, mini-bosses and bosses stay dead and are recorded; ordinary enemies return on room re-entry. In the code every dead enemy is simply freed and nothing is recorded.

**Observed gaps**
- **No vertical pursuit.** An enemy can stand under a platform the Seeker is on, chasing in x only. `INTENT UNKNOWN`.
- **No line of sight.** An enemy notices the Seeker through walls within range. `INTENT UNKNOWN`.
- **Hit-stop overlap.** Each killing blow sets `Engine.time_scale` to 0.05 and restores 1.0 after its timer; two kills within the window can restore normal speed early. Untested. `INTENT UNKNOWN`.
- **No art, animation or audio.** Placeholder boxes, a health bar and a name label only; the telegraph is colour, lean and a "!".
- **No tests.** No test file references the enemy.
- **Tutorial Waster and mini-boss** (the guarded foes in the concept) do not exist.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verification: **Logic** unless marked; tests under `tests/unit/enemies/` for the table and formulas, and an integration scene for behaviour (gdUnit4 not yet installed). Values are read from the `.tres` files.

1. **AC-1 Table.** `transition(from, event)` returns the listed next state for every pair in `TRANSITIONS`, the same state for an unlisted pair, and `DEAD` has no exit.
2. **AC-2 Patrol.** An enemy with `patrol_speed > 0` turns after `patrol_distance` from home, waits `patrol_pause_time`, and turns at a wall or a ledge; a Brute (speed 0) stands still.
3. **AC-3 Sight.** A Grunt notices the Seeker at dx 139 and dy 39, and not at dx 141 or dy 41; a dead Seeker is never noticed.
4. **AC-4 Chase.** A chasing Grunt gives up at dx > 240; it never runs off a ledge.
5. **AC-5 Attack gating.** A wind-up starts only with dx ≤ `attack_range`, dy within `sight_height` and the cooldown clear.
6. **AC-6 Telegraph and hit.** No damage is dealt during the wind-up; the hitbox is live for exactly `attack_active_time`; one `take_hit` per attack even with a continuous overlap; the damage equals `attack_damage`.
7. **AC-7 Cycle.** The wind-up, active, recovery and cooldown times equal the config (F4) for each type.
8. **AC-8 Being hit.** A hit reduces health by the damage and staggers for `hurt_time` with knockback; a Brute hit during its wind-up or attack takes damage but is not staggered; a hit during HURT restarts it.
9. **AC-9 Death.** At zero health from any state: DEAD, `died` emitted once, the hurtbox leaves `hittable`, `Engine.time_scale` returns to 1.0, the body is freed after the fade, and no further hit is accepted.
10. **AC-10 No contact damage.** Overlapping a non-attacking enemy for 5 s deals no damage; the body mask is the world only.
11. **AC-11 No guard by default.** An enemy with no `guard_config` has `guard == null`.
12. **AC-12 Pogo.** A down attack that hits an enemy hurtbox launches the Seeker (a `hittable` target).
13. **AC-13 Data-driven (Config).** All numbers above come from the `.tres` files; changing one changes the behaviour with no code edit.
14. **AC-14 Leash (required; fails today).** A chasing ground enemy never leaves `spawn_x ± (patrol_distance + chase_leash)`, including during knockback and a lunge, and walks back to its spawn point after losing the Seeker.
15. **AC-15 Return delay (open).** An enemy knocked out of its room reappears at its spawn point after 2 s.
16. **AC-16 Persistence (open).** The tutorial Waster, mini-bosses and bosses stay dead after defeat and are recorded in world state; an ordinary enemy returns on room re-entry.
17. **AC-17 Readability (playtest, Visual/Feel).** The owner fights each type and can read and dodge every wind-up, and the Brute's attack cannot be interrupted but can be dodged. Evidence in `production/qa/evidence/`.
