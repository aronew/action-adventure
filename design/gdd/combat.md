## Overview

The Seeker's melee is a 3-hit ground combo, an up attack, two air attacks (jump
and down) and a charged heavy. The down attack is the pogo: a hit bounces the
Seeker upward and refreshes the air moves. The attacks are driven by the player
controller (`Player` decides when an attack may start) and run by `PlayerCombat`,
which plays the body and effect layers in step, switches each attack's hitbox on
only for its hit frames, and reports each hit once per swing. Every number lives
in `attack_data.tres`; the code holds no gameplay literals.

**Design intent** (serves the concept's pillars 1 and 4):
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the combo has no grace window; you chain by pressing during a swing and the combo restarts at attack1 once a swing ends without a queued press.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the uncharged heavy (30) is a deliberate risk-and-reward option; the charge adds the 2x multiplier and, in the guard-break system, the break.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the dash doubling as an attack is **planned, not built**. Today only the pogo does a second job. This is the concept's pillar 4 promise and an open item here.
- `INTENT UNKNOWN — inferred from implementation, not confirmed`: the pogo exists as a traversal tool that uses a combat hit (`pogo_speed` 260, down attack damage 8, the lowest of all attacks).

**Scope**: in: the 7 attacks, input resolution (tap, hold, queue), the heavy charge, hit reporting, the lunge, the pogo, screen-shake requests. Out: guard meter and finisher (Guard-break), enemy health and flinch (Enemies), the Seeker's own health (Health, damage and death), hit-stop and hit effects (Hit feedback), animation content (`seeker_animations.json`).

## Detailed Design

### Attacks

All frame numbers are sheet frame indices from the animation guide. The hitbox is measured from the feet point (y up is negative), written for a player facing right and mirrored when facing left.

| Id | Used when | Damage | Hit frames | Hitbox (x, y, w, h) | Chains into / from frame | Cancel from (`move_cancel_from`) | Lunge px/s | Shake (px, s) |
|----|-----------|-------:|-----------:|---------------------|-------------------------|----:|---:|------|
| `attack1` | Ground tap | 10 | 3-4 | -8, -29, 74, 34 | `attack2` from 5 | 5 | 60 | none |
| `attack2` | Chained from attack1 | 12 | 1-2 | -8, -42, 64, 47 | `attack3` from 4 | 3 | 70 | none |
| `attack3` | Chained from attack2 | 20 | 5-6 | 20, -22, 72, 27 | none (ends the combo) | 7 | 100 | 3, 0.15 |
| `up_attack` | Ground tap with up held and not on a door | 10 | 3-4 | -23, -76, 49, 47 | none | 5 | none | none |
| `jump_attack` | Air press | 10 | 3-4 | -8, -36, 70, 37 | none | 5 | none | none |
| `down_attack` | Air press with down held (the pogo) | 8 | 3-4 | -21, -9, 48, 41 | none | 5 | none | none |
| `heavy` | Ground hold of 0.14 s or more | 30 (60 charged) | 5-6 | -21, -67, 103, 72 | none | 7 | 140 on release | none |

### Input resolution

1. **Ground press.** A press starts a pending state. Released before `hold_threshold` (0.14 s) it is a tap: `up_attack` if up is held and no door is overlapped, else `attack1`. Held for `hold_threshold` it starts the heavy.
2. **Air press.** Swings at once: `down_attack` if down is held, else `jump_attack`. The air never charges a heavy and never chains.
3. **Queued press.** A press during a swing (not on the frame the swing started) is queued with an age. From the attack's `chain_from` frame (or `move_cancel_from` when the attack has no `next`) the queue is used: a ground swing with the button still held waits until the queue is `hold_threshold` old, then starts the heavy; if the button was released first it starts the `next` attack. An air swing never uses a queue for a heavy.
4. **Turning.** A chained swing or heavy turns the Seeker toward the held direction first; otherwise facing is locked for a swing.

### Heavy charge

The heavy starts a wind-up played at `windup_speed_scale` 2.5, holds on frame 4 (`charge_hold_frame`), and the charge timer starts at `hold_threshold` and counts up. At `charge_time` 0.55 s the heavy is **charged**: `heavy_ready` is emitted once, a glow burst and a bright flash play on the Seeker. Release on the button-up strikes from frame 5 (`heavy_release_frame`); a release during the wind-up strikes once the hold frame is reached. While charging the Seeker is planted and skids to a stop at 2000 px/s². `heavy_charged` stays true through the strike so the guard-break can read it.

### Moving while attacking

Dash, jump and grapple are blocked until the attack reaches its `move_cancel_from` frame (`is_locked`), except the heavy's wind-up and charge, which never lock. During a swing the Seeker keeps running; with no input the forward step glides out at 500 px/s². A ground swing's lunge raises forward speed to at least the lunge value when there is floor below. Walking off an edge, landing from an air attack, dashing, jumping, a hit or any state change away from the attack state cancels the attack at once (the effect layer finishes on a detached copy; a queued press is dropped).

### The pogo

A `down_attack` hit sets the Seeker's vertical speed to -260 (`pogo_speed`) and refreshes the air moves. While a down attack plays, the fall cap drops from 480 to 220 px/s (`down_attack_max_fall_speed`) so the hit frames meet what is below. A faster fall is not cut at once: it is moved toward the cap at `gravity x fall_gravity_multiplier` = 900 x 1.5 = 1350 px/s², so a fall at 480 px/s reaches 220 after about 0.19 s (12 physics ticks).

**Load-bearing values.** Ability gating (F1, F7), level design (rule 24) and the registry (`pogo_far_ext`, `pogo_clearance_px`, `hazard_pit_depth_min`) are derived from the down attack's hitbox and `pogo_speed`: forward reach 27 px, back reach 21 px, 32 px below the feet (hitbox -21, -9, 48, 41), launch 260 px/s, and a rise of about 37.6 px (`260² / (2 x 900)`). A change to any of these, or to the controller's `gravity` or `fall_gravity_multiplier`, changes which gates leak: run `/propagate-design-change` before editing them.

### Contract with targets

A hittable is an `Area2D` on physics layer bit 8 in group `hittable`. `PlayerCombat` emits `hit_landed(target, attack, damage)` once per target per swing; `damage` already includes the charged multiplier. Listeners apply it: `EnemyBase` calls `take_hit(attack, damage, charged, from_x)`, passing the hit to its guard component when it has one. A swing also emits `camera_shake_requested` once at its first hit frame when its shake size is above 0 (attack3 only).

## Formulas

**F1. Damage of one hit**
`damage = attack.damage × (charged_damage_multiplier if heavy_charged else 1.0)`

| Variable | Range / value |
|----------|---------------|
| `attack.damage` | 8-30 (table above) |
| `charged_damage_multiplier` | 2.0 |
| `heavy_charged` | true only for a heavy; false for every other attack |

Examples: attack3 = 20; heavy released early = 30; heavy released charged = 60; a full combo (10 + 12 + 20) = 42. Observed context, not a rule: skitter 50 HP, grunt 85, brute 220, boss 600 (before the guard's damage rules).

**F2. Charged state**
`heavy_charged = (hold_threshold + time in the CHARGE phase) ≥ charge_time`, evaluated each physics tick while the phase is CHARGE. The timer starts at `hold_threshold` when the heavy begins, so the heavy becomes charged `charge_time - hold_threshold` = 0.41 s after the CHARGE phase starts. For a press held from the start that is 0.55 s after the press; for a press queued during a swing the heavy starts later than the press, so its time from the press is longer. The check runs before the release is processed on the same tick, so a release is judged on the previous tick's state.
Range: `charge_time` must exceed `hold_threshold` (0.55 > 0.14) or the heavy is charged at the instant it starts.
Example: a heavy released 0.30 s after the CHARGE phase starts deals 30; one released at 0.45 s deals 60 (charged at 0.41 s, about 25 ticks at 60 Hz).

**F3. Hit window**
A hitbox is active while `hit_from ≤ body.frame ≤ hit_to`; each target is reported once per swing (the target list clears when a swing starts).
Range: `hit_from ≤ hit_to` for every attack (the current windows are 2 frames wide). Example: attack1 (`hit_from` 3, `hit_to` 4) is active on sheet frames 3 and 4 only; a target first overlapping on frame 5 is not hit.
Open measurement: Godot may report overlaps for a newly enabled `Area2D` one physics tick after it is enabled, so a hit may register one tick after `hit_from`. Tests allow ±1 tick at the window edges until this is measured.

**F4. Hitbox placement**
`position = (rect.center.x × facing, rect.center.y)` relative to the feet point; `facing` is 1 or -1.
Example: attack3's rect (20, -22, 72, 27) has its centre at (56, -8.5); facing left the box sits at (-56, -8.5).

**F5. Pogo**
On `down_attack` hit: `velocity.y = -pogo_speed` (-260), a rise of `pogo_speed² / (2 x gravity)` = 260² / 1800 = about 37.6 px. While `down_attack` plays and the Seeker is falling: the fall cap is 220 instead of 480, and a faster fall is moved toward it at `gravity x fall_gravity_multiplier` (1350 px/s²), not cut at once.
Range: `pogo_speed` and `down_attack_max_fall_speed` are positive; a cap above `max_fall_speed` (480) has no effect.

**F6. Lunge**
`velocity.x = facing × max(velocity.x × facing, lunge)`, only with floor below. The lunge never slows the Seeker: it is a floor on forward speed.
Range: `lunge` 0-140 (0 or absent means none). Example: attack1 (lunge 60) from rest gives 60 px/s; while running forward at 140 px/s it leaves 140; while moving backward at -50 it gives +60.

**F7. Chain gate**
`open_from = chain_from if present else move_cancel_from`; a queued press acts when `body.frame ≥ open_from`, so a press queued earlier in the swing waits for that frame.
Example: attack1 opens at frame 5 and attack2 at frame 4; attack3 has no `next`, so a press queued during it waits for its `move_cancel_from` (7) and then, with no next hit, until the swing ends.

## Edge Cases

**Handled in the code**
- A press queued during a swing with no next hit (attack3, or any single swing) is held until the swing ends, then starts a fresh ground attack. It carries its age, so a still-held press counts as already held for that long when the next poll starts.
- A press on the same physics frame as a swing start is not queued (prevents a double trigger).
- Dash and grapple pressed during the lock window are dropped (not buffered); a jump press is buffered by the controller's jump buffer and fires once the lock lifts. After `move_cancel_from` all three cancel the attack.
- A cancelled swing leaves its effect finishing on a detached copy and drops any queued press.
- Up held on a door does not choose the up attack (up enters the door).
- A hittable hit by several overlapping swing frames is reported once.
- A heavy released early in the wind-up strikes only once the charge-hold frame is reached.
- No lunge is applied with no floor below.
- Air swings never start a heavy, even with the button still held.

**Not handled / observed gaps**
- **Dash deals no damage.** `INTENT: planned, not built` (owner, 2026-11-22). No hitbox, damage or `hit_landed` exists for the dash.
- **No hit-stop on player hits.** Only enemy death triggers hit-stop (Enemies). `INTENT UNKNOWN`.
- **The `_hit_targets` list holds `Area2D` references.** A target freed mid-swing leaves a stale entry until the swing ends; no error occurs in the code as read, but it is untested.
- **No combat tests exist.** `tests/unit/` holds only terrain tests; `tests/integration/` covers terrain, display and the art-style rooms (ledges, wall slides), none of them combat.

## Acceptance Criteria

Verification: all are **Logic** unless marked; they need unit tests under `tests/unit/combat/` (gdUnit4 is configured but not installed). Test IDs are suggested, not existing.

1. **AC-1 Tap.** A ground press released at 0.10 s starts `attack1`; released with up held and no door overlapped starts `up_attack`; with a door overlapped starts `attack1`.
2. **AC-2 Hold.** With the button down on physics ticks 1 to 9 (0.15 s at 60 Hz), `heavy` starts on tick 9; with the button up on tick 9 or earlier (0.133 s or less of hold), the press is a tap and starts `attack1`.
3. **AC-3 Combo.** A press queued at any point during `attack1` starts `attack2` once the body frame reaches 5 (`chain_from`), and not before; a press queued during `attack2` starts `attack3` at frame 4; `attack3` never chains into a second hit. A press that arrives only after the swing ended starts `attack1`, not `attack2`.
4. **AC-4 Damage.** `hit_landed` reports 10 / 12 / 20 for the three combo hits, 10 for up and jump, 8 for down, 30 for an uncharged heavy and 60 for a charged heavy.
5. **AC-5 Charge.** `heavy_charged` becomes true 0.41 s (about 25 physics ticks) after the CHARGE phase starts, which for a press held from the start is 0.55 s after the press; `heavy_ready` is emitted exactly once, and the flag is still true during the strike. A heavy released before that deals 30.
6. **AC-6 Once per swing.** A target overlapping a hitbox for the whole hit window produces one `hit_landed`; the same target in a later swing produces one more.
7. **AC-7 Hit window.** No hit is reported on a frame outside `hit_from..hit_to`, including frame `hit_from - 1` and the frame after `hit_to`; a hit may register up to one physics tick after `hit_from` (see F3, open measurement).
8. **AC-8 Mirroring.** With the Seeker facing left, each hitbox is mirrored about the feet point.
9. **AC-9 Pogo.** A `down_attack` hit sets `velocity.y` to -260 and refreshes the air moves; a `jump_attack` hit does not.
10. **AC-10 Fall cap.** A down attack started at a fall of 480 px/s decelerates toward 220 at 1350 px/s² and is at or below 220 after 12 physics ticks; one started at 150 px/s accelerates no further than 220.
11. **AC-11 Lock.** Before `move_cancel_from`, dash and grapple presses are dropped (not buffered) and a jump press is buffered and fires once the lock lifts; from `move_cancel_from` all three cancel the attack. This holds for every attack except the heavy's wind-up and charge, which never lock.
12. **AC-12 Cancel.** Walking off an edge, landing from an air attack or starting a dash ends the attack, clears `current_attack` and `heavy_charged`, and drops a queued press.
13. **AC-13 Air.** An air press never starts a heavy and never chains, even held for 1 s.
14. **AC-14 Lunge.** A ground attack1 with floor below sets forward speed to at least 60; with no floor below it changes nothing.
15. **AC-15 Shake.** Only `attack3` emits `camera_shake_requested` (3, 0.15), once per swing.
16. **AC-16 Targets.** Only `Area2D` nodes in group `hittable` on layer bit 8 are reported; a node in neither is ignored.
17. **AC-17 Dash (current state).** The dash emits no `hit_landed`. *This criterion documents today's behaviour and is to be replaced when the dash attack is designed.*
18. **AC-18 Data-driven (Config).** Setting `attack1.damage` to 11 in `attack_data.tres` makes `hit_landed` report 11 for attack1, with no code edit (smoke check).
19. **AC-19 Glide and charge deceleration.** With no input during a ground swing, forward speed decays at 500 px/s²; during the CHARGE phase it decays at 2000 px/s² and left and right input is ignored until the release.
20. **AC-20 Heavy lunge.** A released heavy with floor below sets forward speed to at least 140; during its wind-up and charge no lunge is applied.
21. **AC-21 Turning.** A swing chained from a queued press, and a heavy started from one, turn the Seeker toward the held direction first; a first swing from a fresh press does not turn.
