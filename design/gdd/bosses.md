## Overview

The boss fight is the concept's signature and the subject of its kill condition: break the guard of a boss who cannot be flinched, then punish. `Boss` extends `EnemyBase`, so it reuses the enemy state machine, the telegraph and the death impact, and it always carries a guard (`guard-break.md`). It adds three telegraphed attack patterns (a slow overhead slam, a fast sweeping lunge and a ground shockwave), a way to choose between them by distance, and a fight trigger. Each pattern ends in a recovery, which is the punish window; hits during a wind-up or strike never stagger the boss, only breaking the guard does. A `BossArena` places the Seeker and the boss in a flat room, shows the boss's health and guard, and reports `boss_defeated`.

**Design intent**
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the implemented boss ("Region Guardian") **is Brun, the Stacker**, region 1's boss, with placeholder art. Brun's look and any special traits come later; the mini-boss (Keeper Tavik) is a separate foe that does not exist yet.
- **Changed by the owner after the first playtest (2026-11-22):** the fight is to become **three stages**, with no health bar (progress is read from the boss's behaviour), each stage ended by a guard-break. Stage 2 adds falling hazards and stage 3 adds small enemies. The boss was found "super easy" with one phase and three slow patterns. The code is still one phase; see "Planned: three stages" below. This replaces the earlier "one phase for now" intent.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the next pattern is chosen **at random** among those in range, never the same twice in a row, for now; each pattern is telegraphed so it can be read. A fixed or weighted order is a tuning option after the playtest.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the shockwave is a **jump check**: only a jump avoids it (the dash has no invulnerability). Story 005's "jump or dash" wording is outdated.
- **From the brief and story 005:** beating the boss sets a flag and play goes on, with no completion screen (owner, 2026-11-18); the fight uses only the Seeker's combat kit; the Seeker dying resets the boss and restarts the fight.
- `INTENT UNKNOWN — inferred from implementation, not confirmed`: the per-pattern numbers (tuning), the distance ranges and the gaps between them, and the boss never giving up the chase.

## Detailed Design

### Fight flow

1. The boss stands still (`PATROL` is overridden to wait). `begin_fight()` wakes it; in the test arena this is called once when the Seeker's x reaches 160 (the arena is 960 x 270; the boss stands at x 820).
2. From then on it hunts: in CHASE it faces the Seeker and, once its cooldown is over, picks a pattern. With none available it walks closer until within `attack_range` (36 px). It never gives up the chase (`lose_range` does not apply) and stands still if the Seeker is dead.
3. A pattern runs wind-up (telegraph) → strike → recovery, then the cooldown (`attack_cooldown` 0.6 s), then the next pick.
4. Breaking the guard moves the boss to STAGGER at once, interrupting any pattern, for the guard's stagger time (4.0 s). After it, the boss waits `post_stagger_cooldown` (1.0 s) before the next wind-up.
5. At zero health it dies and the arena reports the defeat.

### The three patterns (`boss_config.tres`)

| Pattern | Chosen when distance is | Wind-up | Strike | Recovery | Damage | Reach |
|---------|-------------------------|--------:|--------|---------:|-------:|-------|
| **Slam** (melee) | 0-64 px | 1.4 s | hitbox live 0.2 s | 1.3 s | 40 | Box 76 x 76 at offset (8, -72) from the feet: 84 px ahead, 72 px high |
| **Lunge** (melee) | 0-200 px | 0.8 s | dash at 320 px/s for 0.5 s with the hitbox live | 1.0 s | 25 | Box 76 x 44 at (0, -40), moving with the boss; 160 px of dash plus a skid |
| **Shockwave** (ranged) | 220-2000 px | 1.0 s | the wave leaves 0.15 s into the strike | 1.0 s | 20 | A 20 x 14 px box on the ground at 150 px/s for 3.5 s (525 px) |

The slam and the lunge use the enemy base's live window and lunge; the shockwave has no melee hitbox, and its "hitbox" is only the lane drawn during the wind-up. The boss works on its own copy of the config and writes the chosen pattern's numbers into the shared attack fields (`apply_pattern`) before each wind-up.

### Pattern choice

When the cooldown is clear: the candidates are the patterns whose distance range holds the horizontal distance to the Seeker, **except the one used last**; one is picked at random. With no candidate the boss walks closer. Consequences, observed from the ranges:
- Within 64 px: slam and lunge are both candidates (one of them excluded after use).
- 64-200 px: only the lunge. After a lunge the boss has no candidate here and walks closer until within 64 px, where only the slam remains.
- 200-220 px: no pattern; it walks closer.
- Beyond 220 px: only the shockwave; after one it has no candidate and walks closer into lunge range.

### Other rules

- **No flinch.** `flinch_during_attack = false`, `hurt_time` 0 and no knockback: a hit during a wind-up or strike deals damage and changes nothing. Only the guard breaking staggers the boss.
- **Shockwave.** It leaves from the boss's front in the facing direction and hits the Seeker once, then vanishes; if the Seeker is invulnerable at that moment the hit does not land and the wave keeps going. It vanishes after 3.5 s or when the boss dies. A wave already in flight continues if the boss is staggered.
- **Health and guard.** 600 health; the guard is built from the boss's own fields (100 meter, x0.2 guarded damage, 4.0 s stagger, 1.5 s recovery, regeneration after 3.0 s at 10 per s; see `guard-break.md`).
- **Body and movement.** A 56 x 84 body, chase speed 55; it never leaves the ground and does not climb.
- **Heal read (all stages) [Owner, 2026-11-23: the boss punishes a planted heal].** When the Seeker starts a heal (`heal_started`, `healing.md`) with the horizontal distance to the boss at most `heal_read_range` (160 px), and the boss is in CHASE, its cooldown or a pattern's recovery, the boss reads it: after `heal_read_reaction` (0.1 s) it drops what is left of the recovery and cooldown and starts a **read lunge**, the lunge with its wind-up shortened to `heal_read_windup` (0.4 s) and everything else unchanged (dash 320 px/s for 0.5 s, damage 25, recovery 1.0 s). A read never starts during a wind-up, a strike, STAGGER or the boss's death, and never more than once per `heal_read_cooldown` (3.0 s, counted from the read's start). The read lunge counts as the last pattern used (F1). If the heal has already ended when the reaction delay is over, the read lunge still starts (the boss committed). The answer the player has: the 0.4 s wind-up is the cue to let go (free, meter kept) and dodge; a heal beyond 160 px or in a stagger is not read. A heal started inside the invulnerability window of an earlier hit (`healing.md` rule 10) is protected for about its first 0.65 s, so a close read lunge can pass through it: the read targets a fresh heal. **Feint:** starting a heal and letting go baits a read lunge and its 1.0 s recovery; it needs a full meter and is limited by `heal_read_cooldown` (a playtest question, `healing.md` Open Question 22).
- **Death.** 1.2 s fade, a 0.25 s game slowdown to 5%, a camera shake of 7 px for 0.4 s, 60 fragments.

### Planned: three stages (owner, 2026-11-22; not built)

A secondary design write-up (wiki-sourced, unverified) describes a four-beat loop (reset and position, tell, short threat, punish) and later stages that compress recoveries or layer hazards instead of adding random moves.

**Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):**
- **Three stages.**
- **A stage ends on a guard-break count:** stage 1 ends at the first guard-break and stage 2 at the second. The guard-break stays the fight's signature move.
- **No health bar.** The player reads progress from the boss's behaviour. The arena's health and guard bars are placeholders and would go.
- **Stage 2 adds falling hazards; stage 3 adds small enemies** (one new element per stage).
- **Design target:** a player needs about 3-4 attempts to learn the moves and beat the boss.
- **A healing system is wanted** (Healing, index row 27, a meter filled by landing hits). The boss must be tuned together with it.

**Proposed, not yet decided (needs the owner):**
- Falling hazards are marked by dust about 1 s before they land; the hazard theme could follow the boss's name (kiln bricks, "the Stacker").
- Stage 2 also shortens the recoveries; stage 3 chains patterns (for example lunge into slam).
- A visible change at each stage transition (a roar, a new look) so the stage is readable without a bar.
- Stage 3's small enemies reuse the Enemies base and config.

`INTENT UNKNOWN`: what happens to the guard meter and the boss's health across a stage change, whether the stage transition replaces the stagger window or follows it, and whether the guard-break threshold changes with each stage.

### The arena (`BossArena`, test room)

A flat code-built room of three solids between two walls. The Seeker spawns at x 48 and the boss at x 820. The HUD shows the Seeker's health, and once the fight starts the boss's name, health and guard (GUARDED, BROKEN or RECOVERING). When the boss dies the arena sets `is_boss_defeated` and emits `boss_defeated`; there is no completion screen. When the Seeker dies the arena reloads after 1.0 s, resetting the boss and the fight, as a stand-in for the save-point respawn.

## Formulas

**F1. Pattern eligibility.** `pattern is a candidate if min <= |dx| <= max and pattern != last`. Slam [0, 64], lunge [0, 200], shockwave [220, 2000].

**F2. Pattern cycle.** `cycle = wind-up + active + recovery`, then `attack_cooldown`: slam 1.4 + 0.2 + 1.3 = 2.9 s, lunge 0.8 + 0.5 + 1.0 = 2.3 s, shockwave 1.0 + 0.15 + 1.0 = 2.15 s, each followed by 0.6 s. The player's reading time is the wind-up; the punish window is the recovery plus the cooldown.

**F3. Lunge reach.** `dash = lunge_speed x active_time` = 320 x 0.5 = 160 px, then the base's skid `lunge_speed² / (2 x hurt_deceleration)` = 320² / 1800 = about 57 px, so the boss ends about 217 px from where it began, against a 200 px choice range; the strike box adds 76 px ahead of the boss.

**F4. Shockwave reach.** `travel = ranged_speed x ranged_lifetime` = 150 x 3.5 = 525 px; the lane drawn in the wind-up is that length from the boss's front.

**F6. Heal read timing.** `t_hit = heal_read_reaction + heal_read_windup + max(0, d - lunge_box_reach) / lunge_speed`, with `t_hit` measured from the heal's press tick, `d` the horizontal distance at that tick, `lunge_box_reach` about 76 px (the strike box ahead of the boss) and `lunge_speed` 320. The read must land before the heal completes at the shortest allowed channel: `t_hit < channel_time` floor 0.9 s (`healing.md` Values). Examples: d 40 px: 0.1 + 0.4 + 0 = 0.50 s (tick 30); d 160 px: 0.1 + 0.4 + 84 / 320 = about 0.76 s (tick 46); d 200 px: about 0.89 s, too close to the floor, hence the 160 px range. The box reach is approximate; AC-20 measures it in a real scene.

**F5. Damage to the boss.** Governed by `guard-break.md` (x0.2 guarded, x1 broken, x2 finisher) and `combat.md`. No time-to-kill is stated here: it needs the attack animation timings, which are not in the source.

## Edge Cases

**Handled in the code**
- Before `begin_fight` the boss does nothing; `begin_fight` is one-way.
- A dead Seeker leaves the boss standing still; the boss never fires `PLAYER_LOST`.
- Hits during a wind-up or strike never stagger it; a hit while it is merely walking sends it to HURT for one physics tick (`hurt_time` 0).
- The guard breaking interrupts any state, including a wind-up or strike, and the hitbox is cancelled.
- A lunge stops at a ledge (base rule).
- A shockwave hits at most once, and keeps travelling through an invulnerable Seeker.
- The boss's death removes every shockwave in flight.
- The Seeker dying reloads the arena and resets everything (stand-in).

**Required by other GDDs, not built**
- **Defeat flag persists** (level design, world state): `boss_defeated` is recorded, survives save and load, and keeps the boss undefeated until set.
- **Reward** (level design rules 10, 21 and Edge Cases): a boss rewards an ability through a pickup that stays in the room until collected; the boss opens doors quietly, with no region-cleared screen. Nothing grants a reward in the code.
- **Save point before the arena** (level design rule 15): respawn at the save point before the boss, not by reloading the scene.
- **A real boss room**, with an antechamber, in the room and grid model (Rooms, Terrain).

**Observed gaps**
- **No Brun identity.** Name, art and any signature move are placeholder (Region Guardian, a purple box).
- **No mini-boss.** Keeper Tavik, who gives the dash, does not exist; the base supports a guard, so the build is data plus patterns (index: mini-boss support inferred).
- **Distance gaps.** No pattern holds at 200-220 px, and a boss that just lunged has none at 64-200 px; it walks closer. `INTENT UNKNOWN`.
- **Random choice cannot be tested deterministically** as written (`pick_random` uses the global generator).
- **No tests.** Story 005 waived tests at `qa.level: minimal`.
- **The kill condition is unchecked.** Two tuning rounds of this fight, and the 10-minute pass test, are still owed to the owner (concept, MVP Definition).

## Acceptance Criteria

Verification: **Logic** unless marked; an integration scene with a stepped physics loop, and a seedable pattern choice (inject the random source). gdUnit4 not yet installed.

1. **AC-1 Trigger.** The boss does nothing before `begin_fight()`; after it, the boss never gives up the chase; with the Seeker dead it stands still.
2. **AC-2 Eligibility.** At 30 px the candidates are {slam, lunge} minus the last; at 100 px {lunge}; at 210 px none (the boss walks closer); at 300 px {shockwave}; never the same pattern twice in a row.
3. **AC-3 Timing.** For each pattern the wind-up, live window and recovery equal the config; no damage is dealt in the wind-up; the live window is exactly `active_time`.
4. **AC-4 Damage.** A slam that lands deals 40, a lunge 25, a shockwave 20, once per strike, through `Player.take_hit`.
5. **AC-5 Lunge.** The dash moves the boss at 320 px/s for 0.5 s and stops at a ledge.
6. **AC-6 Shockwave.** The wave leaves at the release, travels at 150 px/s, vanishes after 3.5 s, hits once for 20 and vanishes; passes through an invulnerable Seeker; a Seeker above 14 px is missed; every wave vanishes when the boss dies; a wave in flight survives a stagger.
7. **AC-7 No flinch.** A hit at any point of a wind-up or strike changes neither state nor position.
8. **AC-8 Stagger.** The guard breaking moves the boss to STAGGER within one tick from any pattern, for 4.0 s; then 1.0 s before the next wind-up.
9. **AC-9 Cooldown.** 0.6 s passes between a pattern's recovery and the next wind-up.
10. **AC-10 Defeat.** At zero health: `died` once, `boss_defeated` once, `is_boss_defeated` true, no completion screen, the death slowdown returns to 1.0.
11. **AC-11 Reset.** The Seeker's death reloads the arena with the boss at full health and not yet engaged (stand-in).
12. **AC-12 Single phase (current behaviour).** The boss's patterns, ranges and timings are the same at 600 and at 50 health. *Documents today's code; replaced by AC-16 to AC-19 when the stages are built.*
13. **AC-13 Data-driven (Config).** Every number above comes from `boss_config.tres`.
14. **AC-14 Persistence (open).** `boss_defeated` survives save and load, and the boss stays defeated; the reward pickup stays in the room until collected.
15. **AC-15 Kill condition (playtest, Visual/Feel).** After two tuning rounds the owner completes the guard-break fight without prompting and would keep playing (the concept's pass test). Evidence in `production/qa/evidence/`. First playtest 2026-11-22: beaten, found too easy, stages requested (tuning round 1).
16. **AC-16 Stage 1 to 2 (planned).** The first guard-break ends stage 1; from the next wind-up the boss is in stage 2 and the falling hazards are active.
17. **AC-17 Stage 2 to 3 (planned).** The second guard-break ends stage 2; stage 3's small enemies appear.
18. **AC-18 No health bar (planned).** No boss health or guard bar is shown; each stage change has a visible and audible cue.
20. **AC-20 Heal read [Integration].** With the boss in a recovery and the cooldown clear of reads: a fresh heal started at 160 px is hit by the read lunge before tick 54 (and before tick 60 at the 1.0 s default), the heal is cancelled with reason `hit` and the meter is 0; the read lunge's wind-up is 0.4 s and starts 0.1 s after the press. A heal at 170 px, a heal during STAGGER and a heal during a wind-up are not read and complete. Two heals started 2.0 s apart within range produce exactly one read lunge; a third 3.0 s after the first produces a second. A heal released 0.05 s after the press still gets the read lunge, and the boss counts it as the last pattern. Positive control: the same scene with `heal_read_range` 0 lets the 160 px heal complete.
19. **AC-19 Learnability (playtest, planned).** A new player beats the boss in 3-4 attempts, and can say after each attempt what killed them (the owner's target, measured over three playtests).
