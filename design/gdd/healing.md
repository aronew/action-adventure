## Summary

Every hit the Seeker lands fills one heal meter; a full meter can be spent on a 1.0 s planted channel that restores 30% of max health. Letting go and every cancel is free and keeps the meter, so a heal costs a second and the damage taken while planted, never the work of earning it. It exists because recovery should be earned by fighting (pillar 1, anti-pillar 2: no consumables), and because the three-stage boss needs a player who can recover between stages.

## Overview

Healing lets the Seeker recover health in the middle of a fight, and it is earned by fighting. Every hit the Seeker lands fills a **heal meter**; when it is full the Seeker can spend it to restore health, as Hollow Knight's Focus does **[Owner, 2026-11-22]**. The player pays with good play (landing hits) and with the second a heal takes, never with an item, a currency or a trip to a save point. The meter is sized from the heal itself (about 4 fill points per HP restored), so a heal costs the same fighting at every max health.

**Where the risk lives (stated once; every other section points here).** Letting go, a jump, dash, grapple or attack press, and in toggle mode a stick push, cancel a heal for free and keep the meter. At today's boss timings a heal started right after a strike ends 0.6 to 0.9 s before the next wind-up begins, so **against the current boss a heal costs a second and a hit that lands, and nothing else**. **[Owner, 2026-11-23]: the stake belongs to the boss, not to the meter.** Healing keeps its rules and **requires** the boss to take that safety away with a pattern or stage element that strikes inside the channel window (Open Question 12, owed to Bosses). **Until it exists, stage 1 of the boss is risk-free by design, and nothing in this document may call a heal a gamble, and AC-26's "felt risky" and "felt fair" questions apply only once the boss supplies the risk.** Without Healing, health is a one-way budget until the next rest, and a long boss fight punishes one early mistake for the whole fight.

**Design intent**
- **[Owner, 2026-11-22]** a meter filled by landing hits; the Seeker can heal when it is full, as in Hollow Knight. Meter size, gain per hit, heal amount, channel time, whether guarded hits count, whether it fills in a boss fight and whether it empties on death are settled below.
- **[Owner, 2026-11-22, first boss playtest]** the boss was beaten and found "super easy"; the owner wants a three-stage boss with no health bar and a heal meter, tuned together (`bosses.md`, "Planned: three stages").
- **From the concept (anti-pillar 2):** a heal is never a consumable, a purchase or a drop. A meter filled by combat fits.
- **[Rec]** pillar 1: recovery is earned by fighting, not by retreating to a save point. **A tension kept on purpose:** any hit fills the meter (a pogo 8, a charged heavy 60), so it does not reward moving through the fight, and the heal is a planted second, the opposite of flowing momentum. The pillar's answer is that cancelling is always free (rule 9).

**Scope**: in: the meter (capacity, fill, keep or empty), the heal action (input, channel, cost, interruption), how health is restored, and the rules for foes and bosses. Out: the health pool, damage and death (R6, code in `player_health.gd`), the meter and health display (HUD, R9), the heal effect and sound (Hit feedback, R8), max-health upgrades (Collectible upgrades) and the boss's stage tuning (Bosses).

## Player Fantasy

*Advisory at `standard`. It holds in full once the boss supplies the risk (Overview).*

- **Ready.** The player is fighting well and feels the bar fill under their hits. When a heal is usable the Seeker carries a quiet mark: *a breath is banked, and I can take it when I choose.*
- **Committing.** Pressing heal is a decision: the Seeker plants and the light gathers for one second, and the player is reading the boss, not the button. Backing out is always allowed (jump, dash, grapple, attack, letting go, and in toggle mode a stick push), so a misread is never a trap, only a wasted second.
- **Being hit.** The light breaks and the hit hurts in full. The player keeps the bar they earned and blames the boss's timing, never the rules.
- **Never.** A consumable (anti-pillar 2), a lock (the player can always leave the channel), or a free refill (the health pool must still matter).
- **Test.** AC-26's playtest asks "felt earned", "felt risky" and "felt fair" in these terms.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**The meter**

1. **One bar.** The heal meter is one number, `heal_meter`, from 0 to `meter_capacity`; one full bar is one heal. **[Owner, 2026-11-22: a bar, a heal spends it all]**
2. **What fills it.** Every hit the Seeker lands on a foe adds `damage` to the meter, where `damage` is the value Combat reports in `hit_landed` for that hit: the attack's base damage with the charged heavy's x2 already in it, **before** the guarded, finisher and edge multipliers. A hit on a guarded foe or boss fills as much as any other. The killing blow counts. One swing that hits two foes adds each foe's own `damage`. **[Owner, 2026-11-22: fill by base damage; Owner, 2026-11-23: guarded hits keep their full fill]**
3. **Only foes feed it.** `hit_landed` fires for anything in the `hittable` group (the `TrainingDummy` and `HitTarget` test nodes included), so a foe is marked explicitly: the hurtboxes of `EnemyBase` (bosses included) and of the `TrainingDummy` join a new group `foe`; `HitTarget` does not. A pogo on a hazard, a breakable or a pane adds nothing. A dead foe's hurtbox has left `hittable` and reports nothing (enemies rule 7). **The `TrainingDummy` stays out of shipped rooms** (AC-42). Whether **stage-3 small enemies** in the boss fight feed the meter is owed to Bosses; the recommendation is that they do not (Open Question 17). **[Rec]**
4. **The meter caps and keeps.** It never exceeds `meter_capacity` (the excess of a hit that crosses the cap is lost), does not decay, and keeps filling at full health, so a player can arrive at a fight with a heal ready. When max health changes, the meter keeps its fraction (F1). "Full" is `heal_meter >= meter_capacity` within a small tolerance (the build notes). **[Rec]**
5. **Resets.** The meter is 0 at a new game, after a Continue and after a death (the `revive` call, which also resets the heal timer and phase). A rest and a room change leave it as it is. It is not saved: like health, it is not in the world-state record (world state rule 1). **[Owner, 2026-11-22]**

**The heal**

6. **Input and start.** A new action `heal`, held to heal (or toggled, rule 14). A heal starts on a press only when all hold: the meter is full; **missing health (max health minus health) is at least `start_deficit`** (F2: `max(1, min(start_deficit_share x heal amount, start_deficit_cap))`, so 15 at 100 max health, 20 from about 133 up, never less than 1, so a heal never starts at full health); and the Seeker's state is `GROUND` (`ATTACK`, `CROUCH`, `HURT`, `DEAD` and the rest are separate states; the landing and standing-up animations are flags inside `GROUND`, so a press during them starts the heal at once). A jump press on the same tick wins (jump is polled first), and a heal cannot start on a tick on which a heal ended (rule 9). The meter is still spent whole on completion (rule 8). **A failed press, and the refusal cue, by priority:** (1) the meter is not full: the **meter** cue; (2) otherwise missing health is below `start_deficit`: the **health** cue; (3) otherwise a state blocks the press: no cue, because the reason is visible on the Seeker (`AIR`, `ATTACK` and `HURT` buffer the press, rule 14). **So a press in the air or mid-combo with an empty meter plays the meter cue.** The meter and health cues play whatever the state, so the most common failure is never silent. **[Rec: ground only, so a heal is a planted commitment; Owner, 2026-11-23: refusal priority changed so an empty meter is always announced]**
7. **The channel.** A started heal is a channel of `channel_time` (1.0 s), the new top-level controller state `Player.State.HEAL`. The Seeker is planted: it skids to a stop on its own `heal_deceleration` (data, default 2000 px/s²) and cannot attack, jump, dash or grapple except to cancel (rule 9). If the skid carries it off an edge, the channel is cancelled and the Seeker is in `AIR` (an involuntary cancel, rule 9); a coyote jump still works. The timer accumulates the physics `delta` of the Seeker's own tick: a pause freezes it and hit-stop stretches it in real time but not in game time; no scene-tree timer and no real-time clock may drive it. **[Owner, 2026-11-22: 1.0 s channel, planted]**
8. **Completion.** When the timer reaches `channel_time` (physics tick 60 at 1/60 s; tick 0 is the press tick, the timer after tick N is N x `delta`) with the heal not cancelled **and health below max health**, the meter is spent in full (set to 0) and health rises by the heal amount (F2), never above max. If health is already at max at that moment (a health step refilled it mid-channel), nothing is healed and the meter is **kept**: the channel ends as `heal_cancelled(&"full_health")`, silent, with no success cue. Nothing is spent before that moment. **[Owner, 2026-11-22: the meter is spent only when the channel completes]**
9. **Cancelling.** A cancel ends the channel with nothing spent, the meter kept, and the time channelled lost (no partial heal). **No cancel has a recovery or a penalty: letting go is always free** **[Owner, 2026-11-23: the late-release recovery and the free window are cut for the first build; Open Question 22 says when to bring one back]**.
    - **Involuntary** (control goes where the event sends it): a hit lands (a hit in the invulnerability window does not land, so it does not cancel) and the Seeker goes to `HURT` or `DEAD`; the Seeker dies; a door, an exit or a rest takes over (`interact` with a door or rest area overlapped); the skid carries the Seeker off an edge (rule 7).
    - **Voluntary, by an action press** (free): jump, dash, grapple (once owned) or attack. The channel ends on that tick and the pressed action starts as it would in `GROUND`; **attack arms Combat's own tap or hold resolution that tick** (a tap swings on release, a hold becomes the heavy). **A press cancels only if the action would start**: a dash on cooldown or a grapple with no anchor in range (or not owned) is ignored and the channel continues. When several presses arrive on one tick, this order wins: grapple, dash, door or rest `interact`, attack, jump. Climb is not polled during a channel, so it neither cancels nor pre-empts the jump. A jump press cancels and jumps exactly once; a jump buffered before the heal started never cancels it.
    - **Voluntary, by letting go:** the heal input is released (hold mode) or pressed a second time (toggle mode). A release on the completion tick does not cancel: the heal completes (rule 15), and in toggle mode so does a second press on that tick.
    - **Voluntary, by a stick push (toggle mode only):** a fresh horizontal push of the stick, D-pad or arrow keys past `stick_cancel_deadzone` cancels free, so a player with no "let go" reflex is never trapped planted. A direction already held at the press does not cancel until it has returned inside the deadzone. Up never cancels (`interact` shares it). In hold mode the stick is ignored and releasing the button is the way out. **[Owner, 2026-11-23, fifth design review: toggle mode only]**
    - **Everything else is ignored and not buffered:** crouch, climb, bow, cast, rapid fire, map, and `interact` with no door or rest area.
    - **A press is spent once.** A `heal` press that starts a heal or cancels one is consumed; a heal that ends on a tick cannot start again on that tick, and a button still held after an action cancel does not restart a heal (rule 14's fresh-press rule).
10. **No protection of its own.** The channel gives no invulnerability, damage reduction or armour. A hit that lands cancels it and deals its full damage. **One accepted exception [Owner, 2026-11-23]:** a heal that starts while the invulnerability window of an earlier hit is still running is protected until that window ends, because a hit in the window does not land. The window (1.0 s) outlasts the hurt lock (0.35 s) by 0.65 s, so such a heal is protected for about 0.63 to 0.65 s of its first second. It is kept because the 0.12 s press buffer after `HURT` makes right after a hit the natural moment to heal. It is a stage-tuning constraint: a boss pattern must strike after the window to punish such a heal (Open Question 12).
11. **Fills during a heal.** The Seeker cannot attack while planted, so nothing fills the meter then; a hit from a delayed source (none today) would still count (AC-34). **[Rec]**

**Boss fights**

12. **The meter fills in a boss fight like anywhere else** (rule 2); the boss's stages are tuned with the heal in mind. The starting values are **not** tuned to a heals-per-cycle target: F3 bounds what a skilled player can earn per guard-break cycle (1 heal in the stagger; 2 when a banked heal is also spent in the guard phase; about 1.5 sustained on the charged-heavy path), and whether that is fair is **decided in play** from the AC-26 log. **The windows a heal fits in** (`bosses.md`): from the end of a strike to the start of the next wind-up is the previous pattern's recovery plus `attack_cooldown` 0.6 s: **1.9 s after a slam, 1.6 s after a lunge or shockwave**; the next strike comes that gap plus the next pattern's wind-up later, and the pattern just used is not picked again, so the gap to the next strike is **at least 2.4 s** (2.4 to 3.0 s). A 1.0 s channel started the moment a strike ends therefore ends 0.9 s (slam) or 0.6 s (lunge, shockwave) before the next wind-up. **A heal that follows a hit starts after the 0.35 s hurt lock**, so it ends about 0.55 s (slam) or 0.25 s (lunge, shockwave) before the next wind-up, and at least 1.05 s before the next strike. After a stagger the next wind-up waits `post_stagger_cooldown` 1.0 s in place of the 0.6 s cooldown (0.4 s more). These are lower bounds: the choice of pattern depends on distance and the boss walks at 55 px/s. **The boss cannot punish a heal at the current timings; that safety is the boss's to take away (Open Question 12).** One exception: a shockwave already in flight (3.5 s at 150 px/s) reaches a Seeker planted at distance d after d / 150 s. **In a stagger (4.0 s)** a heal costs 1.0 s of punish damage on the boss's health, but the planned stages end on a guard-break count, so until the last stage that damage is not what progress needs and a heal in a stagger is nearly free (owner-accepted, watched in the AC-26 log). **[Owner, 2026-11-22: tune the boss together with healing]**
13. **A stage change neither fills nor empties the meter.** What the transition does for the player's heal is Open Question 2.

**Input and timing**

14. **Press buffer and hold or toggle.** *Press buffer:* a `heal` press that fails only because the Seeker is in `ATTACK`, `HURT` or `AIR` (a state that ends on its own) is remembered for `press_buffer` (0.12 s). If every start condition of rule 6 holds within that window the heal starts on that tick, the test being `press age <= press_buffer` (a `press_buffer` of 0 still starts on the press tick). **In hold mode the input must still be held** (a tap released inside the window starts nothing); **in toggle mode it need not be**, because a toggle press is a tap. A press that fails on the meter or the health is never buffered. *Hold or toggle:* an accessibility option `heal_mode` (Open Question 14): in `hold` mode (default) the channel needs the input held and releasing cancels; in `toggle` mode a press starts the channel, it runs without being held, and a second press cancels as a release does. Every other cancel and completion are the same in both modes. **Toggle mode must ship with the heal** (controls cannot be remapped in the first scope, `pause-and-menus.md` rule 17). *Detection:* a start press uses the fresh-press test (including a latch that catches a tap shorter than one physics tick) and a hold-mode release is detected by a held-state poll, never by an edge, because an edge is lost across a pause; a `heal` press on the first tick after the game resumes from a pause is not a heal press. A press held longer than `press_buffer` when conditions begin to hold does not start a heal: the player releases and presses again. A press that fails to start does not arm the toggle. How these are built is the implementation notes and ADR-0003. **[Rec; Owner, 2026-11-23: buffer fixed for toggle mode]**
15. **Order within a physics tick (outcomes).** Hitters act first, then the Seeker, so **a hit that lands on a tick always wins over a heal that would complete on that tick** (AC-18), and a hit that lands after the Seeker's own tick still cancels before the heal is applied. On each tick after the press tick: (a) a channel found in `HEAL` has not been hit; (b) a cancel press is applied, and a release or toggle second press is applied **unless this is the completion tick** (the timer plus one `delta` would reach the completion time); (c) the timer advances; (d) completion is tested. **The tick-60 asymmetry is on purpose:** a release or second press on the completion tick completes the heal, and a jump, dash, grapple or attack press on it cancels and the heal is lost. **How the order is guaranteed is `docs/architecture/adr-0003-heal-tick-order.md`** (the owner chose a priority-10 helper node on 2026-11-23, so Combat and Enemies are not changed).

### States and Transitions

The meter has no states: "full" means `heal_meter >= meter_capacity`. The heal is one new top-level controller state, `Player.State.HEAL`, with one phase (the channel). `IDLE` means the Seeker is not in `HEAL`. Cleanup (timer, effects, the pending-completion flag) runs on any exit from `HEAL`.

| Phase | Enters when | Leaves when |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| `IDLE` | Start of play; a heal completes or is cancelled | A fresh press (or a buffered press) with rule 6 holding: to `CHANNEL` |
| `CHANNEL` | Rule 6 holds on a press | Timer reaches `channel_time` uncancelled: to `IDLE` after the heal (or with nothing spent at max health). An action press that would start, a release, a toggle second press or a toggle stick push before the completion tick: to `IDLE` at once. A hit, death, door, rest or edge slide: to `IDLE` and the event's state |

| Exit | Meter | Health | Player state after |
|------|-------|--------|--------------------|
| Completes (health below max) | 0 | + heal amount (capped) | GROUND, control the same tick |
| Completes with health at max | kept | unchanged | GROUND, `heal_cancelled(&"full_health")` |
| Release, toggle second press, toggle stick push | kept | unchanged | GROUND, control the same tick |
| Jump, dash, grapple or attack that would start | kept | unchanged | the action starts the same tick |
| A hit lands | kept | - the hit's damage | HURT (or DEAD) |
| Slide off an edge | kept | unchanged | AIR |
| Door or rest takes over | kept | unchanged | ENTER_DOOR or the rest |
| Death | 0 on revive | 0 | DEAD |

## Formulas

Values are data (a `HealingConfig` resource), never constants in code. The figures they act on come from `attack_data.tres` (damage 8-30, charged heavy x2), `player_config.tres` (`max_health` 100, up to 180 with collectible upgrades) and the enemy and boss `.tres` files. Starting values approved by the owner on 2026-11-22. Every variable below was worked at its minimum, maximum and 0 in the fifth review.

### F1. Meter fill and capacity

`heal_meter' = min(heal_meter + max(damage, 0), meter_capacity)`, with a non-finite `damage` adding nothing

`meter_capacity = max(capacity_floor, meter_cost_ratio x heal_share x max_health)`

When max health changes (a collectible step completes) the meter keeps its fraction: `heal_meter' = heal_meter x new_capacity / old_capacity`, so a full bar stays full and a half bar stays half (a step is skipped if `old_capacity` is 0). **[Rec; Owner, 2026-11-23, for the derived capacity]**

| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| heal_meter | float | 0 to `meter_capacity` | calculated | The meter before the hit; 0 at a new game, a Continue and a death |
| damage | float | 8-60 | Combat `hit_landed` | The hit's base damage, with the charged heavy's x2, before the guarded, finisher and edge multipliers |
| meter_capacity | float | 84 to 720 | calculated | 120 at 100 max health and the defaults, 216 at 180 |
| meter_cost_ratio | float | 2.8-10, starts at 4.0 | data | Fill points per HP restored (0.25 HP per fill point at 4.0, the same at every max health). The lower bound is the capacity floor at the defaults (below 2.8 the floor applies). The upper bound is about 6 s of perfect light combos per heal at the defaults (300 / 49), the longest a heal should cost in a boss fight **[Rec]** |
| capacity_floor | float | 60-120, starts at 84 | data (validated) | The lowest capacity: two combos (84), above a charged heavy (60). At 0 or below "full" would always be true and fraction-keeping would divide by zero, so `validate()` covers it |
| max_health, heal_share | float | 100-180, 0.20-0.40 | F2 | Read as in F2 at the moment the meter fills or its capacity is shown |

**Output range:** 0 to `meter_capacity`. **Example:** meter 100, a charged heavy (30 x 2 = 60): `min(100 + 60, 120)` = 120, 40 lost. At 180 max health the capacity is 216. A first combo from empty: 10, 22, 42.

### F2. Heal amount and start deficit

`health' = min(health + heal_share x max_health, max_health)`

`start_deficit = max(1, min(start_deficit_share x heal_share x max_health, start_deficit_cap))`

| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| health | float | above 0 up to `max_health` | `PlayerHealth.current` | Health when the channel completes; a heal never starts at 0 or at full |
| max_health | float | 100-180 | collectible upgrades F2, registry `max_health_derived` | Read at completion, never copied |
| heal_share | float | 0.20-0.40, starts at 0.30 | data | Below 0.20 a heal is too small to be worth a planted second; at 0.40 on 180 max health it is 72, just under two slams (80); above about 0.44 it is more than two slams |

**Output range:** `heal_share x max_health`: 30 at 100 max health, 54 at 180, 20 to 72 across the ranges; the cap makes the gain smaller near full health. The heal is 30 HP of a 40-damage slam at the start, 54 HP at full upgrades (1.8 times the worth, Open Question 6). **A minimum-deficit heal wastes more:** at 100 max health a heal at the 15 HP threshold uses a 30 HP heal for 15 (50% wasted) and burns 120 fill for it (8.0 fill per HP); at 180 max health the threshold is 20 of 54 (63% wasted, 10.8 fill per HP). The "same cost at every max health" holds for full heals only. **Example:** health 70 of 100: 100. Health 80: capped at 100 (10 of the 30 unused). At 180 max and health 90: 144.

### F3. Hits per heal and the boss-cycle check

A tuning check, not a runtime calculation.

`hits_per_heal = ceil(meter_capacity / mean_hit_damage)`
`meter_at_stagger = min(start_meter + guard_fill, meter_capacity)`, where `start_meter` is 0 if the banked heal was spent in the guard phase (it needs the meter full and a safe 1.0 s there), else `carry_in`; a bar carried in full and not spent wastes the whole guard phase's fill
`t_full = max(0, (meter_capacity - meter_at_stagger) / fill_rate)`
`stagger_heals = max(0, floor((stagger_time - t_full + meter_capacity / fill_rate) / (channel_time + meter_capacity / fill_rate)))` (channels are serial and each needs a full refill; 0 when the stagger is shorter than `t_full + channel_time`; piecewise at `fill_rate` 0: 1 heal if the bar is already full and `stagger_time >= channel_time`, else 0)
`cycle_heals = guard_heals + stagger_heals`, with `guard_heals` 1 when the meter is full at the start of the guard phase and the player spends it in a safe 1.0 s there. **Not a target**
`heal_nominal = heal_share x max_health`; `heal_effective` is the health actually gained; `heal_wasted = heal_nominal - heal_effective`; `heal_ratio = heal_effective_per_cycle / boss_damage_taken_per_cycle`: logged, never a target. Health cannot exceed max, so in steady state `heal_ratio` is at most about 1; **it cannot detect a heal that is too strong**, because it is capped by construction. What the playtest reads is `heal_wasted`, how often a full bar sits unusable, the heal's timing, and the heal income against the boss's damage budget (Open Question 24).

| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| mean_hit_damage | float | 8-60 | calculated | 14 for light combos (10, 12, 20), 30 for uncharged heavies |
| carry_in | float | 0 to capacity | calculated | The meter at the start of the guard phase: 0 (spent) or full (not) in steady play |
| guard_fill | float | 60-120 | calculated | Fill from a full guard to the breaking hit. **Three paths:** 106 for steady light combos (8 hits, `guard-break.md` F2); **120 for four uncharged heavies** (4 x 30, a full bar at the break, the whole 4.0 s stagger to heal in); 60 for a charged heavy, which breaks the guard in one hit and is the path the first boss playtest used |
| fill_rate | float | 0 upward | estimated | About 49 per second for light combos (a 3-hit combo of 42 in about 0.86 s). **An estimate, not measured**; the heavy's and the pogo's rates are not estimated (pogo about 20/s) |
| stagger_time | float | 2.5 s default, 4.0 s boss | `guard_config.tres` | Hits do not extend it |

**Output range:** `hits_per_heal` is 2 (two charged heavies) to 15 (pogo at 8) at the defaults; **2 to 50 across the capacity range at 100 max health** (capacity 400, pogo 8) and up to 90 across the whole range (720 / 8). `cycle_heals` and `heal_ratio` have no target band **[Owner, 2026-11-22: determined by play]**.

**Example 1, boss, light-combo break, estimate** (guard breaks on the 8th hit, `guard_fill` 106, stagger 4.0 s, 100 max health). *Banked heal not spent (carry_in 120):* the whole 106 is lost, `stagger_heals` = floor((4.0 + 2.45) / 3.45) = 1; **1 heal per cycle**, a full meter carried on. *Spent in the guard phase (carry_in 0):* meter 106 at the break, full 0.29 s in, `stagger_heals` = floor((4.0 - 0.29 + 2.45) / 3.45) = 1 (the first channel ends at 1.29 s, leaving **2.71 s**, less than the 3.45 s a second heal needs); with `guard_heals` 1: **2 heals per cycle**. *HP:* two heals are 60 HP nominal, but health cannot exceed max: after one slam (40) at 100 max health the guard-phase heal takes health 60 to 90, and the stagger heal then has 10 missing, below `start_deficit` (15), so it cannot start; `heal_ratio` is about 1.0.

**Example 2, charged-heavy break:** `guard_fill` 60, meter 60 at the break, full 1.22 s in (60 / 49); the player heals 1.2 to 2.2 s and refills about 88 (0.73 of a bar) in the remaining 1.8 s; next cycle: `meter_at_stagger` = min(88 + 60, 120) = 120 (28 lost), one stagger heal, a full bar carried out. The path alternates 1 and 2 heals: **about 1.5 sustained**. A player who does not heal in this stagger loses about 137 of fill.

**At 180 max health** (capacity 216): from a carry_in of 0 the guard phase (106) leaves `t_full` 2.2 s and the stagger heal runs from 2.2 to 3.2 s of 4.0 s. **Max-health bound (Open Question 18):** a stagger heal from empty fits on the light path up to about 210 max health (capacity about 253), but on the **charged path** only while capacity <= 207, so max health <= 172 at a 4.0 s stagger (at 180 `t_full` is 3.18 s and `stagger_heals` is 0 from empty), and <= 111 at a 2.5 s stagger.

`HealingConfig.validate()` returns the name of every value outside its range (all nine above). The caller reports a non-empty result through an injected callable and clamps each value on a duplicate of the loaded resource, never on the shared cached `.tres` (AC-24; the build notes). **[Rec]**

**Feedback loops:** one positive loop, intended: a heal keeps the player alive longer, so they land more hits and earn more heals. Its dampers are that a heal restores at most the health the player is missing, and a boss's health is a finite stock. **Neither is a damper against heal strength:** effective healing cannot exceed damage taken by construction, which says nothing about whether health is effectively unlimited. On paper (the economy lens, fifth review) heal income while attacking is about 12 HP/s (0.25 HP per fill point x 49 fill/s), about 9 to 10 HP/s with channels, against a boss whose unavoidable damage is about 34 HP per 7.7 s guard-break cycle even if the Seeker never dodges, and the boss's damage does not scale with max health. **That is an owner decision for Bosses (Open Question 24), not a Healing number.** Deriving the capacity from the heal keeps HP per fill point the same at every max health, **not the pool share**: a heal at 180 max health costs 216 fill (4.4 s of hitting, 1.35 slams) against 120 (2.45 s, 0.75 slams) at 100. **Seam:** collectible upgrades F3 raises sword health damage by up to x1.3; this system reads the damage **before** that multiplier, so edge steps do not make a swing fill faster, although kills then take fewer swings, so fill per kill falls by up to about 23%.

## Edge Cases

Every case is a **[Rec]**; the system is not built, so none is "handled in the code".

**Starting and ending a heal**
- **Pressed with the meter full and health full:** nothing starts or is spent; the health cue plays. The meter stays full.
- **Pressed with the meter below capacity** (in any state): nothing starts; the meter cue plays (rule 6). Never buffered: a press 0.1 s before the meter fills starts nothing.
- **Pressed with missing health below `start_deficit`** (health 86 to 100 of 100): nothing starts; the health cue plays. A heal at 85 of 100 or lower starts.
- **Pressed in `ATTACK`, `HURT` or `AIR`** with meter and health conditions met: remembered for `press_buffer`; if rule 6 holds in that window (and in hold mode the input is still held) the heal starts on that tick. A press during the landing or standing-up animation starts at once.
- **Held longer than `press_buffer` when the conditions began to hold:** no heal; release and press again.
- **Pressed crouched, on a ladder, grappling, on a wall, dashing, on a ledge or dead** with meter and health met: nothing starts, nothing is buffered, no cue (the reason is visible on the Seeker).
- **Pressed during a door transition or a rest:** the controller has locked input, so the press never reaches the heal logic: no start, no cue.
- **Inside the invulnerability window with the hurt lock over:** a heal may start; the first 0.63 to 0.65 s are protected (accepted, rule 10).
- **`interact` in a door or rest area while channelling:** the door or rest takes over and the channel is cancelled, the meter kept.

**During the channel**
- **A hit lands on the tick the timer reaches `channel_time`:** the hit is resolved first, the channel is cancelled, no heal (rule 15).
- **`heal` released on the completion tick:** the heal completes. A jump, dash, grapple or attack press on it cancels and the heal is lost.
- **Released before the completion tick (or a toggle second press or stick push):** the Seeker is in `GROUND` that tick, no cost, meter kept, time lost.
- **An action press that would not start** (dash on cooldown, grapple with no anchor): ignored; the channel continues.
- **The skid carries the Seeker off an edge:** cancelled (involuntary), `AIR`, coyote jump available.
- **A hit arrives in the invulnerability window** (`take_hit` returns false): not cancelled, since no damage was dealt.
- **Max health changes mid-channel:** F2 reads max health at completion; the capacity is recomputed and the meter keeps its fraction. If the step refilled health to max, completion heals nothing, spends nothing and emits `heal_cancelled(&"full_health")`.
- **The Seeker dies mid-channel:** cancelled; the meter is zeroed on `revive`.
- **Paused, or the time scale drops:** the timer accumulates the physics `delta`, so a pause freezes it and hit-stop stretches it in real time but not in game time; it neither completes nor cancels while paused. **Requirement:** `Engine.time_scale` is restored from a static counter, not from a coroutine on a foe that can be freed, and overlapping hit-stops are counted (ADR-0003). The Player stays pausable.
- **A foe the Seeker cannot see lands a hit:** it cancels like any hit.
- **`heal_mode` is `toggle`:** a press starts the channel and it runs without the input held; a second press or a fresh stick push is a cancel. A press that fails to start does not arm the toggle, a press consumed by the pause menu is not a heal press, and a second press on the completion tick completes the heal.

**Filling**
- **One swing hits two foes:** each adds its own `damage`.
- **The killing blow lands:** its damage counts, once.
- **A pogo hits a foe:** it adds 8, the lowest fill of any attack. On a hazard, breakable or pane it adds nothing.
- **The meter is full and more hits land:** the excess is lost, no cue.
- **The Seeker dies in the tick it lands a hit:** the meter is zeroed on `revive` regardless.
- **`damage` is 0, negative or not finite:** it adds nothing; the meter never falls from a hit.
- **`validate()` finds a value out of range:** it is reported and clamped; the game keeps running.

**Boss and balance**
- **Healing in a stagger (4.0 s):** allowed, and nearly free until the last stage (rule 12). A choice, not an exploit; whether it is too cheap is what the log reads.
- **The Seeker dies to the boss and the fight resets:** the meter is 0 on revive; the next attempt starts with no heal.
- **A boss stage change mid-channel:** it cancels nothing here; a hit that lands as part of it cancels as usual (Open Question 2).
- **Farming (known loop, accepted for now):** a foe rebuilt on room re-entry (rooms rule 12) can be killed again and again to fill the meter; Hollow Knight has the same loop. A rest itself is never farmed (save points rule 3a keeps enemies away from rest areas). On paper looping one rebuilt foe with no damage taken yields about 6 to 12 bars of fill a minute (720 to 1440 fill at 100 max health); the health gained is bounded by the missing HP (recovering from empty takes about 3.3 heals, roughly 30 to 60 s of looping), so this is a rest-point substitute, not unbounded income. The paper figure already exceeds the old trigger of 240 fill a minute, so the trigger is restated as a per-spawn kill count in Open Question 3.

**Flash limit.** The pulse, the bloom, the refusal luminance cue, the cancel fade, the glow and the meter's fill pulse count toward registry `flash_limit` (3 per second, WCAG 2.3.1) **together**, with the invulnerability blink and the hurt effect on the same screen area measured in the same capture. A luminance event is a pair of opposing changes of at least 0.10 relative luminance (absolute, WCAG's reading); the registry states the limit as "10% relative luminance" and the unit is to be confirmed against `pause-and-menus.md` F4 (Open Question 26). With Flash reduction On the heal's lights read as steady glows and the refusal cue is a positional nudge. HDR output must be off or capped, and the renderer setting is recorded with the capture, because with HDR on a value above 1.0 can display brighter than SDR white. With the atmosphere set to Reduced (parallax rule 14) the heal still reads through the pose, the marker and the meter, not through bloom.

**Audio event contract and owner.** Gameplay behaviour, fixed here: the ready tone fires only on a 0-to-full crossing caused by a landed hit; the swell starts on `heal_started`, follows `heal_progress` (so a pause freezes it, and it never follows `time_scale` or pitch) and stops within 80 ms of every exit; every heal audio cue has a visual twin and none is the only carrier of information. The channel swell must not share the shape of a boss attack charge-up, so Bosses owes a transient-onset telegraph sound outside the swell's band (art bible l.120 already requires enemy telegraphs to carry a sound; `bosses.md` has none: Open Question 25). Owed to the Audio GDD (index #25, Open Question 19): mix priority and ducking, ready-tone anti-fatigue (it can fire every 10 s in a boss fight), a sound per cancel exit, loudness targets, bus and naming, the audio acceptance criteria.

**Animation.** `seeker_animations.json` has no heal clip. New clips `heal_loop` (planted pose, loops) and `heal_end` (cosmetic: control returns on the completion tick and any input interrupts it). Frame counts are the animator's, never tied to `channel_time`. Build details: the implementation notes.

**HUD colours** (for the HUD GDD): the meter and its full state use no key colour and no red against green; the full state is a shape or brightness cue, not a hue change alone.

## Game Feel

**Feel reference.** Hollow Knight's Focus: a held, planted commitment that a hit breaks. The difference here is that the meter is kept on a cancel, so a mistake costs time and damage, not the work of earning the meter.

| Target | Value | Why |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Press to `CHANNEL` | the same physics tick | A heal the player chose must start when pressed; the wait is the channel, not input lag |
| First visible response | within 1 tick | The player sees the commitment begin |
| Cancel to control | By an action press that would start, by letting go, by a toggle second press or stick push: 0 s. After a hit: the controller's hurt lock | Cancelling is always free, and a slip costs nothing |
| Completion to control | 0 s | The heal is the reward; no extra lock |
| Refusal echo | every refused (a) or (b) press, within 1 tick (the luminance cue and the audio tick are limited, the echo is not) | A press that did nothing must be seen to do nothing |
| Ready cue | the tick the meter reaches capacity | The player learns a heal is **banked** without looking away |
| Press buffer | a press up to `press_buffer` before the Seeker can heal starts the heal on the first tick it can | After a hit or a swing the first instinct is to press heal; losing it feels like a bug |

**Weight.** The channel is a planted 1.0 s with no armour. Against the current boss the only risk is a hit that lands and a shockwave already in flight (rule 12); in a stagger a heal is nearly free. A heal adds no hit-stop or screen shake. These targets are checked in the owner's playtest (AC-26); "feels earned" is not a threshold.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Conventions.** Defaults are the starting values at player `max_health` 100: `meter_capacity` 120, `heal_share` 0.30, `channel_time` 1.0 s, `start_deficit` 15, `press_buffer` 0.12 s, `run_speed` 140 (read from `player_config.tres`), run against a pinned fixture `tests/fixtures/healing_config_test.tres` (a boss fixture pins the guard values for AC-23). Tags: **[Logic]** unit test of the pure `HealChannel` or `HealMeter` (implementation notes: one input record per tick); **[Integration]** real nodes and the physics server (a hitter's overlap is established at least one step earlier; a pause test uses a real `get_tree().paused`); **[UI]** retained screenshot or capture in `production/qa/evidence/`; **[Playtest]** owner playtest. A physics tick is 1/60 s, tick 0 is the press tick, the timer after tick N is N x `delta`, every timing is counted in ticks, and float comparisons use a tolerance. Every "no cue" assertion is paired with a positive control. A test that reads `heal` calls `PlayerInputBindings.register()` first. **Not closable by Healing's story:** AC-9b, AC-9c, AC-20c, AC-32b (they close with the Continue, new game, rest and room-change flows), and **AC-25, AC-27b to 27e, AC-31, AC-37** (they need the HUD GDD, the heal clip, a flash-analysis tool in `tools/` or the boss stages). A story may not close on a stub that passes vacuously. The first built story keeps the standard's launch-and-observe evidence: a retained screenshot of the placeholder heal pose in the `TrainingDummy` room in `production/qa/evidence/`.

- **AC-1 Fill [Logic].** GIVEN the meter is 0, WHEN attack1, attack2 and attack3 land on a foe (10, 12, 20), THEN the meter reads 10, 22, 42. **AC-1b:** a pogo (8) on a foe adds 8.
- **AC-2 Cap [Logic].** GIVEN the meter is 100, WHEN a charged heavy lands (60), THEN 120; a further hit leaves 120 and `meter_full` was emitted exactly once across both.
- **AC-3 Guarded hits fill in full [Integration].** GIVEN the boss is GUARDED and the meter 0, WHEN attack1 lands, THEN the meter is 10 and the boss's health fell by 2.0 (within tolerance). **AC-3b:** a finisher (attack3 on a BROKEN foe, damage x2 on the health) still fills 20, not 40.
- **AC-4 Only foes feed it [Integration].** One test per target. GIVEN the meter is 30, WHEN the down attack hits (a) a hazard, (b) a breakable, (c) a pane, (d) a `HitTarget`, THEN 30. **AC-4b:** an `EnemyBase`, a boss hurtbox or the `TrainingDummy` (group `foe`) adds the hit's damage.
- **AC-5 Killing blow [Integration].** A foe with 5 health, meter 0, attack1 kills it: the meter is 10, added once.
- **AC-6 One swing, two foes [Integration].** Two foes under one attack1 swing, meter 0: 20.
- **AC-7 Bad damage [Logic].** Meter 50; a hit of damage 0, then -5, then NaN: 50 after each.
- **AC-8 No decay [Logic].** Health 100 of 100 and meter 60: after 3600 ticks of the pure meter with no hit, 60; **AC-8b:** a hit then raises it.
- **AC-9 Resets [Logic; 9a and 9d run today].** Meter 80: **9a** a death and `revive` leave 0; **9b** a Continue and a new game each leave 0; **9c** a rest and a room change each leave 80; **9d** the meter is not in the saved record.
- **AC-10 Not ready [Logic].** Each case with the meter full unless stated. WHEN `heal` is pressed: **10a** meter 119: no `HEAL`, nothing spent, the meter cue; **10b** health 86 of 100: no `HEAL`, the health cue; **10c** health 85: `HEAL` starts; **10d** one test for each of `CROUCH`, `CLIMB`, `WALL_SLIDE`, `DASH`, `GRAPPLE`, `LEDGE` and `DEAD` with health 70: no `HEAL` and **no cue**; for `AIR`, `ATTACK`, `HURT` the press is buffered and the state is held at least 8 ticks (or the input released) so the buffer expires; **10e** (priority): meter 50 with health 100 gives the meter cue only; meter 50 **in `AIR` gives the meter cue**; meter 120 with health 100 in `AIR` gives the health cue; meter 120 with health 70 in `CROUCH` gives no cue; **10f** (cap, max health 180): health 161 refused, 160 starts; **10g** (floor): `start_deficit_share` 0, health 100 refused, 99 starts; **10h** a jump press on the same tick as a heal press jumps and no heal starts; **10i** a press during the landing or standing-up flag starts at once.
- **AC-11 Fresh press and buffer [Logic].** **11a** `heal` held 30 ticks through an attack, meter full: no heal when the attack ends; released and pressed again: `HEAL` starts. **11b** (hold mode) pressed 7 ticks before an attack's lock ends and still held: starts on the first `GROUND` tick; released first, or pressed 8 or more ticks before: nothing (the boundary is between 7 and 8 ticks). **11c** the meter reaches capacity 3 ticks after a press: no heal. **11d (toggle)** a tap pressed and released 5 ticks before the lock ends: the heal starts when the lock ends. **11e** `press_buffer` 0 still starts on the press tick; a press that failed on health is never buffered.
- **AC-12 Planted [Integration].** The Seeker runs at `run_speed` with a full meter and health 70, `heal` is pressed: horizontal speed is 0 by tick 5, the horizontal displacement from the press is at most 8.5 px (the skid is about 5 px; the bound allows tick 0 to move at full speed), and from tick 6 the x position does not change (assert x only: gravity jitters y).
- **AC-13 Completion [Logic].** Meter 120: **13a** health 70 of 100, `heal` held to tick 60: health 100, meter 0, back in `GROUND` on tick 60; **13b** health 50: 80; **13c** max health 180, meter 216, health 90: 144, meter 0; **13d** health 85: 100, meter 0.
- **AC-14 Not before time [Logic].** A channel started on tick 0: after tick 59 the meter is still 120 and health unchanged, and the heal applies on tick 60 exactly. **14b:** with variable deltas summing to 1.0 s (for example 0.3 + 0.7) it completes on the tick the sum reaches 1.0.
- **AC-15 Letting go [Logic].** **15a (hold)** a release on any tick 1 to 59: `GROUND` that tick, health unchanged, meter 120, no recovery. **15b** a release on tick 60 completes the heal (health 70 to 100 in the fixture, meter 0). **15c (toggle)** a second press at tick 30 cancels, `GROUND` that tick; a second press on tick 60 completes. **15d** a cancel at tick 30 then a fresh press: the heal completes 60 ticks after the new press.
- **AC-16 Action cancels [Integration].** One test per action. A channel at tick 30; **16a** jump, **16b** dash, **16c** grapple (once owned): the channel ends that tick, the action starts that tick, meter 120, health unchanged; **16d** attack: `GROUND` that tick and Combat has a pending attack (a tap swings on release, a hold becomes the heavy; asserted through the Combat accessor); **16e** dash pressed during its cooldown, and a grapple with no anchor: the channel continues; **16f** the order grapple, dash, interact in a door area, attack, jump wins on one tick, adjacent pairs table-driven.
- **AC-17 A hit cancels, no protection [Integration].** **17a** a channel at tick 30 and a foe attack of 15: health falls by 15, meter 120, no heal, `HURT`. **17b** a full meter, a first hit of 15 or more on absolute tick 0, a fresh press on tick 22 (past the 21-tick lock), a second hit at channel tick 30 (absolute 52, inside the 60-tick window): `take_hit` returns false, the state is still `HEAL`, meter 120, health unchanged, timer running. **17c** the second hit at channel tick 40 (absolute 62): `take_hit` returns true, `HURT`, meter kept. **17d** the first channel tick on which a second hit lands is measured in the first story and asserted (about 38 for a press on tick 22).
- **AC-18 Hit and completion on one tick [Integration, real SceneTree].** A channel and a hitter whose overlap was established at least one step earlier; the hitter's `take_hit` runs on tick 60: the hit lands first, the channel is cancelled, no heal, meter 120, `HURT`. One test per caller: **18a** an `EnemyBase` melee hit, **18b** the boss's melee hit, **18c** the boss's `Shockwave`; each with the hitter before and after the Seeker in the tree, using a probe node that records call order. **18d** a static test greps `src/` for `take_hit(` callers and fails on any not on the allowlist (`EnemyBase`, the boss, its `Shockwave`) and on any deferred `take_hit` (ADR-0003). **18e** a release on tick 60 completes the heal; a jump press on tick 60 cancels it and the jump starts.
- **AC-19 Death [Logic].** **19a** a channel and a killing hit: cancelled; after `revive` the meter is 0 and the timer and phase are reset. **19b** the Seeker lands a hit and dies on the same tick: the meter is 0 after `revive`.
- **AC-20 Interact takes over [Integration].** **20a** a channel in a door area, `interact`: the door takes over, the channel is cancelled, meter kept; **20b** `interact` with no door or rest area is ignored; **20c (not closable until the rest flow exists)** the same in a rest area.
- **AC-21 Max health changes mid-channel [Logic].** A channel and a health step completes (max 100 to 120, health set to 120): at completion no heal is applied, `heal_cancelled(&"full_health")` is emitted and no `heal_completed`, the meter is kept (144: the capacity rose to 144 and the fraction is kept).
- **AC-22 Pause [Integration].** A channel at tick 36, the game paused for 300 frames with `heal` held (the action stays pressed) and resumed (real `get_tree().paused`): the timer is approximately 36 x `delta` after the pause, the heal completes on the 24th tick after the resume, `can_process()` was false while paused, and a static grep finds no `create_timer`, `Timer` node or `Time.get_ticks_*` in the heal path.
- **AC-23 Boss cycle arithmetic [Integration].** Default config and a full-guard boss fixture: attack1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 3, 1, 2 break the guard on the 8th hit with the meter at 106; attack3 in the stagger then gives 120 (126 capped). **23b:** meter 0 and a charged heavy breaks the guard with the meter at 60. **23c:** four uncharged heavies break the guard with the meter at 120. How many heals a cycle gives is AC-26.
- **AC-24 Config validation [Logic].** `meter_cost_ratio` 1.0, `heal_share` 0.6, `channel_time` 3.0, `start_deficit_cap` 0, `capacity_floor` 0 and `stick_cancel_deadzone` 0: `validate()` returns those six names; the injected `report` callable receives them and each value is clamped on a duplicate (2.8, 0.40, 2.0, 5, 60, 0.2) with the cached resource unchanged. **24b:** the boundary values of every range return an empty list. **24c:** just outside a bound (`meter_cost_ratio` 2.79 and 10.01) returns that name and is clamped.
- **AC-25 Meter display [UI, needs the HUD GDD].** A retained screenshot shows the meter at 0, 60 of 120, banked-and-usable, banked-not-usable and each refusal family. A still cannot show a 0.2 s shudder, so AC-27 covers its motion.
- **AC-26 Boss-cycle log and playtest.** **26a [Logic]** a `HealLog` writer with an injected tick clock and path writes the header `tick,event,source,max_health,attack_id,amount,lost,health,meter,spawn_id` and one row per event from a fixture: `fill`, `meter_full`, `heal_started`, `heal_completed`, `heal_cancelled`, `damage_taken`, `kill`, `died`, `guard_broken` and, when the stages exist, `stage_changed`. `source` names where each fill came from (first kill, re-entry kill, boss guard phase, boss stagger); `lost` is the overflow; `health` at `heal_started` gives the missing health at heal start. `heal_nominal`, `heal_effective`, `heal_wasted`, `fill_rate` per attack path, fill per minute by source and the heal income against damage taken are computed at report time, never asserted. **26a-int [Integration, TrainingDummy]** one full heal writes exactly one `heal_started` and one `heal_completed` row. **26b [Playtest, advisory, a milestone read at the vertical-slice `/gate-check`, never a story gate]** the one-stage boss is enough to start: the owner plays three attempts and a report in `production/qa/evidence/` holds the log, heals earned and spent per cycle, the F3 metrics, a read at 100 and at 180 max health, a yes or no on "felt earned", "felt risky" and "felt fair" (the last two apply once the boss supplies the risk), and the owner's recorded decision. No number is a pass-fail threshold **[Owner, 2026-11-22]**.
- **AC-27 Flash limit and cue limiters [27a Logic; 27b to 27e UI, retained capture].** **27a** ten refused presses at ticks 0, 6, 12, ..., 54: ten `heal_refused` signals, **exactly 2 luminance cues (ticks 0 and 30)** and **exactly 5 audio ticks (0, 12, 24, 36, 48)**, counted in integer ticks. **27b** a scripted second with 10 refused presses, and a second with 5 start-and-cancel cycles (presses at ticks 0, 12, 24, 36, 48, each released 6 ticks later), plus a combo of 6 hits filling the meter, captured at 60 fps in a rendering run (not headless): in any 1 s window at most 3 luminance events occur counting the refusal cue, glow, pulse, bloom, fade, the meter's fill pulse, the invulnerability blink and the hurt effect together. **27c** with Flash reduction On the same captures show 0 luminance events. **27d** the pulse and bloom peak at luma 0.64 or less and never reach 0.93. **27e** the glow is never Kiln Amber, ice-white or a key colour. *Measurement:* the region is the Seeker's sprite rectangle plus the meter; the 27d caps use the art bible's luma; the event count uses WCAG relative luminance (an event is a pair of opposing changes of at least 0.10); HDR output is off; the renderer setting is recorded. Sign-off by the lead.
- **AC-28 Catch-all and the jump buffer [Logic for 28a, 28c to 28d; 28b, 28e Integration].** **28a** a channel at tick 30: crouch, climb, bow, cast, rapid fire, map, and in hold mode a stick push: the channel continues. **28b** jump at tick 30: the channel ends that tick and exactly one jump happens. **28c** a jump pressed in a state that cannot consume it (for example `ATTACK` or `HURT`) 3 ticks before the Seeker becomes `GROUND` with the heal press buffered: the jump buffer is zero when `HEAL` starts and the state is still `HEAL` after its first tick. **28d** a hit cancels the channel on the tick a jump is pressed, with the hurt lock more than 8 ticks from ending: the jump buffer is 0 at exit and no jump fires. **28e** see AC-16f.
- **AC-29 Stick cancel [Integration].** **29a (toggle)** a fresh horizontal push at tick 30 cancels free: `GROUND` that tick, meter kept, no restart while the button is held; **29b (hold)** the same push does nothing and releasing cancels; **29c** a direction already held at the press does not cancel until it has returned inside the deadzone; **29d** an Up push never cancels.
- **AC-30 Edge slide [Integration].** The Seeker skidding at a ledge so the skid carries it off: the channel is cancelled (`heal_cancelled(&"edge")`), `AIR`, meter kept, a coyote jump works, and gravity applied in `HEAL` (the Seeker falls on the tick after the floor is lost).
- **AC-31 Heal visuals [UI, needs the heal clip].** Retained screenshots show the ready pulse and the persistent marker (with its Void Black outline at the measured contrast), the channel glow at ticks 0, 30 and 59 with a visible luma delta on the body, the completion bloom and the `heal_end` settle, each within the art bible's colour limits, lead sign-off.
- **AC-32 Locked input [Integration].** **32a** during a door transition, **32b** (not closable until the rest flow exists) during a rest: `heal` starts no `HEAL`, the meter is unchanged and no cue plays.
- **AC-33 Start tick and the spent press [Logic].** **33a** with every start condition holding, after the press tick the state is `HEAL`, `heal_started` was emitted once and `heal_refused` was not. **33b (toggle)** a second press at tick 30: `GROUND` and no new channel on that tick or while the button stays held. **33c** a channel cancelled by a dash at tick 30 with `heal` still held: no heal restarts until a fresh press.
- **AC-34 Fill during a heal [Logic].** A channel and the meter at 60: an injected `hit_landed` on a `foe` target raises the meter by its damage and the channel continues.
- **AC-35 Toggle mode [Integration].** **35a** `heal_mode` toggle, `heal` pressed at tick 0 and released at tick 1: the channel continues and completes at tick 60. **35b** a second press at tick 30 cancels free. **35c** a second press on tick 60 completes the heal. **35d** a press that fails to start (meter 119) does not arm the toggle: the next press with the meter full starts a heal rather than cancelling one.
- **AC-36 Release while paused [Integration].** A channel in hold mode, the game paused, `heal` released during the pause, the game resumed: the channel is cancelled on the first tick after the resume, and a `heal` press on that first tick is not a heal press.
- **AC-37 Stage change [Logic, blocked until the stages exist and Open Question 2 is settled; owner: Bosses].** The meter is 80 when `stage_changed` is emitted: 80.
- **AC-38 Capacity derivation [Logic].** `meter_capacity` is 120 at max health 100, 216 at 180, never below 84 (`heal_share` 0.20 and `meter_cost_ratio` 2.8 at 100 give 56, so the floor applies). Max health 100 to 120 with the meter at 120: capacity and meter 144; with the meter at 60: 72. With `old_capacity` 0 the rescale is skipped.
- **AC-39 Signals [Logic, recording listener].** A hit taking the meter from 119 to 120 emits `meter_full` once, and none on a further hit, on `revive`, a load, a rest or a room change. A channel emits `heal_started` on tick 0 and exactly one of `heal_completed(effective)` or `heal_cancelled(reason)` on its last tick for **every exit**, the reason being one of `release`, `hit`, `death`, `door`, `rest`, `edge`, `jump`, `dash`, `grapple`, `attack`, `stick`, `full_health`. `heal_progress` is 0.5 at tick 30 and is frozen while paused. Ten refused presses (meter not full) emit `heal_refused(&"meter")` ten times; a refusal in a movement state with meter and health met emits nothing (positive control: the same press in `GROUND` without a start emits).
- **AC-40 Refusal and echo [Logic].** Every refused (a) or (b) press gets the non-luminance echo event within 1 tick; the (c) family gets none.
- **AC-41 Shipped config [Logic, advisory smoke check].** The shipped `healing_config.tres` equals the documented defaults and passes `validate()`.
- **AC-42 Test nodes out of shipped rooms [static].** No shipped scene contains a `TrainingDummy`.

**Gaps found by the lens (not blocking):** rule 11 has no source today (AC-34 injects one); rule 13 cannot be tested until the stages exist (the AC-26a log records the meter at a stage change); "a breath won back by pressing the attack" is a feeling, AC-26b's question; `tests/unit/healing/` and `tests/integration/healing/` do not exist yet; the pre-edge damage seam (Open Question 4) has no criterion until an edge step can complete.
