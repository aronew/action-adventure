## Overview

Guard-break is the game's boss signature (concept: *Unique Hook*, pillars 1 and 4, and the MVP kill condition). A guarded foe shrugs most hits off: each hit drains a guard meter and deals reduced damage. When the meter empties, or a fully charged heavy lands, the guard breaks: the foe is staggered, takes full damage, and the Seeker's `attack3` and `heavy` become finishers worth double. After the stagger the guard recovers and the cycle restarts.

It is a self-contained node, `GuardComponent`, added as a child of a foe. The foe feeds it every hit with `take_hit(attack, damage, charged)` and the component ticks itself. All numbers live in a `GuardConfig` resource (`guard_config.tres`), which the boss builds from its own `BossConfig`.

**Design intent**
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the hit that breaks the guard lands at full damage (x1) and is not itself a finisher. The finisher is the next hit: break first, then punish. A charged heavy that breaks the guard deals 60; the finisher bonus starts with the following hit.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** the default `guard_config.tres` values are the template for the tutorial foe and mini-bosses; the boss is a tuned variant with a longer window and harder guard (x0.2, 4.0 s, regen after 3.0 s).
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** regeneration keeps the pressure on: retreating or stalling undoes progress, so the player must stay in the fight.
- **Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-22):** `attack2` costs 10 guard while dealing 12 damage, deliberately (every other attack costs exactly its damage). The reason was not stated: `INTENT UNKNOWN` beyond "deliberate".
- `INTENT UNKNOWN — inferred from implementation, not confirmed`: the boss uses x0.2 where the default is x0.25; whether guarded foes should be affected by player hit-stop.

**Scope**: in: the three guard states, the meter, the multipliers, finishers, regeneration, the config. Out: the Seeker's attacks (Combat), enemy state machines and the boss's attack patterns (Enemies, Bosses), the guard UI (HUD), shatter and spark effects (Hit feedback). Normal enemies have no guard by rule (concept anti-pillar 1).

## Detailed Design

### States

| State | Meter | Hits do | Ends when |
|-------|-------|---------|-----------|
| `GUARDED` | Starts full (100). Drains per hit by `guard_damage[attack]` | Health damage x `guarded_damage_multiplier` | The meter reaches 0, or a charged heavy lands |
| `BROKEN` | 0 | Full health damage; finishers x `finisher_bonus_multiplier` | `stagger_time` elapses |
| `RECOVERING` | Refills from 0 to full over `recovery_time` | Full health damage; nothing happens to the guard | `recovery_time` elapses, then `GUARDED` with a full meter |

The whole transition table is `TRANSITIONS`: GUARDED `--guard broken-->` BROKEN `--stagger ended-->` RECOVERING `--recovery ended-->` GUARDED. An event not listed for a state changes nothing.

### Rules

1. **Guarded hit.** Resets the regeneration timer. Cost is `guard_damage[attack]` (an id with no entry costs 0). The meter is clamped at 0. Health damage is `damage x guarded_damage_multiplier`.
2. **Breaking.** The guard breaks when the meter reaches 0 after a hit, **or** when the attack is `heavy` and `charged` is true, whatever the meter holds. The meter is then set to 0, `guard_broken` is emitted once, and the breaking hit deals **full** damage (x1).
3. **Broken.** Every hit deals full damage. An attack in `finisher_attacks` (`attack3`, `heavy`) deals `x finisher_bonus_multiplier` (2.0). Hits do not extend the stagger.
4. **Recovering.** Hits deal full damage with no finisher bonus. They do not change the meter or the state. A charged heavy does not break a recovering guard.
5. **Regeneration.** While guarded, after `regen_delay` seconds without a hit the meter gains `regen_rate` per second, up to `max_guard`. Any hit resets the delay. There is no regeneration in the other two states.
6. **Health.** The component keeps its own `health` (clamped at 0) and emits `damaged(amount, health_left)` on every hit, `amount` being the damage after the multipliers.

### Values (`guard_config.tres` default / boss)

| Value | Default | Boss |
|-------|--------:|-----:|
| `max_guard` | 100 | 100 |
| `guard_damage` attack1 / attack2 / attack3 | 10 / 10 / 20 | 10 / 10 / 20 |
| `guard_damage` jump / up / down / heavy | 10 / 10 / 8 / 30 | 10 / 10 / 8 / 30 |
| `guarded_damage_multiplier` | 0.25 | 0.2 |
| `stagger_time` | 2.5 s | 4.0 s |
| `finisher_bonus_multiplier` / `finisher_attacks` | 2.0 / attack3, heavy | 2.0 / attack3, heavy |
| `recovery_time` | 1.0 s | 1.5 s |
| `regen_delay` / `regen_rate` | 2.0 s / 20 per s | 3.0 s / 10 per s |
| `max_health` | 200 | 600 |

### Who uses it

- **`EnemyBase`** creates a `GuardComponent` only when `guard_config` is set (bosses and mini-bosses, by rule). It routes each player hit to `guard.take_hit` and uses the returned `damaged` amount as its health damage. `guard_broken` fires its `GUARD_BROKEN` event and moves the foe to `STAGGER`, which interrupts a wind-up, a strike or a chase. The foe leaves `STAGGER` on its own timer, `guard.config.stagger_time`.
- **`Boss`** builds its guard from `BossConfig.make_guard_config()`. Hits during a wind-up or strike do not stagger the boss; only breaking the guard does. After a stagger it waits `post_stagger_cooldown` (1.0 s) before the next wind-up.
- **`TrainingDummy`** (test room) carries a guard and refills its health at 0.

## Formulas

**F1. Guarded hit**
`meter' = max(meter - guard_damage[attack], 0)`; `health_damage = damage x guarded_damage_multiplier`, unless the guard breaks (then `x1`).
Examples (default): attack1 (10) → meter 90, damage 2.5; attack3 (20) → meter 80, damage 5; boss attack1 → damage 2.0.

**F2. Break condition**
`break = (charged AND attack == "heavy") OR meter' <= 0`
Example: from a full meter, a steady 3-hit combo costs 10 + 10 + 20 = 40, so the guard breaks on the **8th** hit (attack2 of the third combo: 10, 20, 40, 50, 60, 80, 90, 100), assuming no regeneration and no pause between hits.

**F3. Finisher**
`health_damage = damage x finisher_bonus_multiplier` for `attack in finisher_attacks` while BROKEN; `damage x 1` otherwise.
Examples: attack3 20 → 40; heavy 30 → 60; charged heavy 60 → 120; attack1 10 → 10.

**F4. Recovery meter**
`meter = max_guard x state_time / recovery_time` while RECOVERING. At `recovery_time` the state becomes GUARDED and the meter is set to `max_guard`.

**F5. Regeneration**
`meter' = min(meter + regen_rate x delta, max_guard)` each tick, while GUARDED and `state_time >= regen_delay` (`state_time` = seconds since the last hit).
Example (default): a meter at 60 after a hit starts regenerating 2.0 s later and is full 2.0 s after that.

Observed context, not a rule: the guard costs equal each attack's damage except attack2 (cost 10, damage 12); boss health 600.

## Edge Cases

**Handled in the code**
- An attack id missing from `guard_damage` costs no guard (and still takes the guarded multiplier).
- A charged heavy breaks a full meter in one hit; an uncharged heavy at a full meter costs 30 and does not break.
- A hit while BROKEN or RECOVERING does nothing to the guard; a charged heavy cannot re-break.
- Meter exactly 0 after a hit counts as broken (`<= 0`).
- Health never goes below 0.
- An event not in the transition table changes nothing, so a late timer cannot corrupt the state.

**Not handled / observed gaps**
- **No guarded foe other than the boss.** The tutorial foe and mini-boss (Keeper Tavik, concept and brief) do not exist. `none found in the source`.
- **No shatter or spark effect.** The concept names a guard-break shatter. Only the debug HUD bars and colours exist (`boss_arena.gd`, `training_dummy.gd`). Belongs to Hit feedback (R8).
- **No dedicated finisher.** A finisher is only a damage multiplier; there is no execution animation or guaranteed kill.
- **Double bookkeeping of health.** `GuardComponent.health` is separate from `EnemyBase.health`; foes use only the `damaged` amount. The component's health matters only for the training dummy. `INTENT UNKNOWN`.
- **Clock.** The component ticks in `_process`, so `Engine.time_scale` slowdowns (enemy-death hit-stop) stretch the stagger and regeneration timers, while the foe's own stagger timer runs in the physics step. The two clocks can drift. `INTENT UNKNOWN`.
- **No tests.** `tests/` holds only terrain tests. The component was written for testing (`take_hit` and `tick` can be called directly).

## Acceptance Criteria

Verification: **Logic** unless marked; unit tests under `tests/unit/guard_break/` (gdUnit4 not yet installed). Call `take_hit` and `tick` directly: no randomness, time enters only through `delta`.

1. **AC-1 Start.** A new component is GUARDED with meter 100 and health equal to `max_health`.
2. **AC-2 Guarded hit.** attack1 on a full default guard leaves the meter at 90, emits `damaged(2.5, …)` and stays GUARDED.
3. **AC-3 Unknown id.** An attack id with no cost leaves the meter unchanged and deals x0.25.
4. **AC-4 Meter break.** Eight steady hits (a1, a2, a3, a1, a2, a3, a1, a2) break a full guard on the eighth; that hit deals 12 (attack2, x1); `guard_broken` is emitted once; the meter is 0.
5. **AC-5 Charged heavy.** A charged heavy (damage 60) on a full meter breaks the guard and deals 60 (x1). An uncharged heavy (30) on a full meter does not break it, costs 30 and deals 7.5.
6. **AC-6 Charged flag alone.** `charged = true` with any attack other than `heavy` does not break the guard.
7. **AC-7 Finishers.** While BROKEN: attack3 20 → 40, heavy 30 → 60, charged heavy 60 → 120, attack1 10 → 10.
8. **AC-8 Stagger.** BROKEN lasts exactly `stagger_time` (2.5 s default, 4.0 s boss) of ticked time and hits inside it do not extend it.
9. **AC-9 Recovery.** At 0.5 s of a 1.0 s recovery the meter is 50; hits deal full damage with no finisher bonus and change neither meter nor state; a charged heavy does not break it; at 1.0 s the state is GUARDED, the meter is 100 and `recovered` is emitted once.
10. **AC-10 Regeneration.** From a meter of 60 after a hit: no change before 2.0 s; then +20 per second, capped at 100; any hit restarts the 2.0 s delay; no regeneration while BROKEN or RECOVERING.
11. **AC-11 Health floor.** Health never falls below 0, and `damaged` carries the post-multiplier amount on every hit.
12. **AC-12 Table.** `transition(GUARDED, STAGGER_ENDED)` returns GUARDED, and every listed transition returns its next state.
13. **AC-13 Boss config.** `BossConfig.make_guard_config()` copies every guard field (100, costs, x0.2, 4.0 s, 1.5 s, 3.0 s, 10 per s, 600).
14. **AC-14 Integration.** On a boss, a hit during a wind-up does not stagger it, and breaking the guard moves it to `STAGGER` for `stagger_time`.
15. **AC-15 No guard by default.** An `EnemyBase` with no `guard_config` has no `guard` and takes the player's damage at x1.
16. **AC-16 Kill condition (playtest, Visual/Feel).** The owner completes one guard-break on the boss without prompting and says they would keep playing (the concept's MVP pass test). Evidence in `production/qa/evidence/`.
