# Region 1: The Cooling Yards

Status: DRAFT. Identity: the long yards behind the kilns where fired work was carried to cool, now a street of glazed couriers still running.

**Proposal (owner may change):** the mentor's hub and the wall-cling shrine are both in region 1, so dash, wall cling and double jump are all earned here. The grapple gate is visible here and opens only after a later region's Keeper.

## Places (what the player sees, not reads)

1. **Waking Niche** (start, save). A cooling niche in a kiln wall, brick warm on the back. A hand-print of the Seeker's own shape pressed in the ash of the niche floor.
2. **Yard Steps.** Glazed figures stand still along the wall, one mid-sneeze. High on the roofline, one small steady window-light no kiln would make.
3. **Saggar Hall** (mini-boss: Brun; the Toppled Stack beyond it). Columns of stacked clay boxes to the ceiling, a loader's ladder lashed to each. Once Brun falls, his column lies as a ramp, and high above it a single flat kiln shelf hangs on three chalk stilts.
4. **Yard Gate** (first dash gap). A broken road. Orange soot streaks run to each lip of the gap, as if something has crossed it a thousand times.
5. **Carrier's Road.** A long street of glazed couriers leaning one way, trays held out, a pot still glowing on each. An iron ring hangs high above, out of reach.
6. **Carrier's Kiln** (Keeper). A cold hearth room, a table of empty trays, one cooling niche with a pot waiting on the sill.
7. **Flue-Sweeper's Bench** (shrine). A stool, a stained apron on a peg, and a tall jar with the last coil unlaid. A left palm-print is pressed into its wall.
8. **Stacking Loft** (mentor hub). A tilted loft over the yards, one lamp of real flame, a loader's apron on a nail, a cup with a thumb-dent.
9. **Flue Run** (shortcut). The Carrier's Kiln's back flue, sooted clean, folding back to the Yard Gate niche.
10. **Ropewalk Stair** (region edge). A stair into a wide drop. An iron ring across the gap, a violet cord trailing from it, and below, rope-wound doors shut.

## The Keeper: Tavik, the Hot-Carrier

- **Who:** head of the carriers, who ran fresh-fired work from kiln to cooling niche before it cracked. Glazed mid-run on the last evening, a pot on his tray he never set down.
- **Craft of passage:** the hot carry, the last few steps when the tray cannot be set down. It becomes the dash.
- **Fight:** short hot bursts tray-first, then stops dead at the end of each. That stop is the opening.
- **When he falls:** the pots on the Road's trays go dark one by one, the soot streaks go cold, and the back flue opens. The couriers keep standing. No banner.

## The mini-boss: Brun, the Stacker

Glazed loader in oxblood, a saggar column on his back, still stacking. Swings and drops the column in sweeps. Has a glaze guard. When he falls, the column topples into the ramp to the Yard Gate. He taught Pell.

## The shrine: Dorrit's unfinished jar

Dorrit Kell swept the tall flues and made jars on the side. She never glazed and died unglazed mid-coil. The Seeker lays its palm into her palm-print and steadies the wall of the jar. The clay takes the hand: wall cling.

## The mentor: Pell

- **Look:** stooped loader, leather apron, cloth-wrapped hands, bad hip, a small lamp.
- **Three facts:** he stacked under Brun; he never glazed and is wearing down (hairline cracks under the wraps); he has seen the Seeker's shape before in Teodra's workshop sketches.
- **Promise (hidden):** see `lore-premise.md`. Pell says only that he will one day ask for something, and the Seeker is to refuse. Never the reason.
- **Double jump:** the loader's second step, pushing off the first board before it stops being a board.

## Save point: the cooling niche

The Seeker sits, back to warm brick. Ash settles. Its pale shade warms a half-step and the nearest glazed figure's highlight slowly dims. It stands up the same as it sat down.

## Ordinary creatures (no guard, health only)

- **Wasters:** lopsided failed pieces, hopping jugs and slumped bowls. Never fired through or glazed, so they chip and die like clay.
- **Cinder moths:** ash-grey moths drawn to the flue-light. Living things; no skin to hold.

Glazed figures that are not Keepers or Brun are inert scenery. Nothing but a boss or mini-boss wears glaze and moves.
